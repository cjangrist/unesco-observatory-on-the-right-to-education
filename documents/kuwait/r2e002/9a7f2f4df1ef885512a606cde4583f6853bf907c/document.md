# Legislative Decree

## Concerning the Ministry of Education

Having considered article 73 of the constitution,

Having considered the Amiral Decree No. 2 of 1962 concerning the reorganization of the ministries and the regulations associated to it,

Having considered the decree governing the formation of the Cabinet issued on February 16th 1978,

In response to the Prime Minister's request,

The Cabinet has passed the following Law which we hereby ratify and promulgate as follows:

## We have resolved the following:

### Act No.1:

The Ministry of Education undertakes the progress of the Kuwaiti society and a full comprehensive spiritual, moral, intellectual, social and physical development of its sons in the light of Islamic principles, Arabic heritage and contemporary culture in accordance to the nature of Kuwaiti society and what guarantees its advancement and development.

### Act No.2

The Ministry of Education is responsible for the following:

- 1- Proposing the general framework for educational policies and plans to be implemented according to the specifications of the Ministry. The Ministry can establish high committees and consultative subcommittees to assess the implementation of these plans.
- 2- Attending to educational affairs and all related matters, which include the determination of the stages of education, development of the curricula and setting the admission policies and the regulations related to exams and approval of local and foreign textbooks.
- 3- Attending to adult education affairs, centers for eradication of illiteracy, public schools, and other technical and religious institutes, as well as giving opinion on the establishment of institutes under other authorities.
- 4- Appointing unemployed students to educational missions and training courses.
- 5- Designing and applying extra-curricular activities in sports, social and cultural fields; and providing students with social care.
- 6- Overseeing Arabic and foreign public schools and institutes according to legislations and laws.
- 7- Cooperating with ministries and authorities concerned with matters of sports, science, culture and youth's care.
- 8- Cooperating in matters of education and culture with Arab, international and foreign countries and organizations.

### Act No.3

The Prime Minister, all Ministers each within his jurisdiction, shall implement this Law Decree which will come into force the time it is published in the Official Gazette.
