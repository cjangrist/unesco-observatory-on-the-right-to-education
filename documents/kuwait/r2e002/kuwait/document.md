# Subject: 11th Consultation on the Implementation of the 1960 Convention and Recommendation against Discrimination in Education

With reference to the subject above, and to the letter from the National Committee addressed to Your Excellency, dated 08/10/2024, with reference number (0409738), concerning the 11th Consultation on the Implementation of the 1960 Convention and Recommendation against Discrimination in Education, which includes a letter from UNESCO regarding the submission of the State of Kuwait's national report in accordance with Article 8 of the UNESCO Constitution,

We hereby inform Your Excellency of the following:

## 1. Promoting Educational Inclusivity:

### Integration of Students with Disabilities:

Implementation of programs to integrate students with disabilities into both public and private schools, while providing educational, psychological, and social support. Tailored and flexible curricula are developed to meet their needs and accommodate the diversity of disabilities.

### Establishment of Specialized Educational Facilities:

The opening of two schools in Sabah Al-Ahmad Residential City — Al-Irada School for Boys and Al-Tahaddi School for Girls — dedicated to serving students with all types of disabilities.

### Infrastructure and Facilities:

The State is committed to providing a safe and stimulating educational environment in kindergartens, equipped with the latest tools and educational resources that foster learning and exploration among children.

Continuous evaluation and quality assurance in early childhood education is conducted through specialized educational bodies and institutions that carry out regular visits to assess performance.

## 2. Combating Discrimination and Ensuring Equality:

### Implementation of Fair Policies:

Private schools are required to enforce equal tuition fees for all nationalities without discrimination.

### Promoting Equal Access to Education:

The Ministry of Education has enabled Kuwaiti women who are married to pursue their education during evening hours. This initiative grants them greater flexibility to care for their children and allows shared caregiving responsibilities within the family.

This is in line with Article (29), which states: "People are equal in human dignity and are equal before the law in public rights and duties, without discrimination on the basis of gender, origin, language, or religion."

### Financial Support for Marginalized Groups:

An annual financial aid of six million Kuwaiti Dinars is provided through the Charitable Fund to support students from the category of residents with unresolved legal status.

### Reinforcement of Non-Discriminatory Fee Policies:

Private schools are again mandated to ensure tuition fees are equal for students of all nationalities, reaffirming the State's stance against discriminatory practices.

### Formation of a Student Fund Committee:

A committee under the Student Fund in Social Services has been established in collaboration with the Zakat House, aimed at providing financial assistance and support to students in need within the State of Kuwait.

## 3. Enhancing the Quality of Education:

### Teacher Qualification:

Great emphasis is placed on teacher qualification through intensive training programs aimed at enhancing student competencies.

### Diversity Training:

Training programs are organized for teachers and administrative staff on managing cultural and religious diversity in classrooms, with the goal of developing an educational environment that respects and values this diversity.

### Monitoring and Evaluation:

- Mechanisms have been established to monitor educational practices, which aid in identifying and addressing any form of discrimination.

## 4. International Cooperation:

### Enhancing Educational Policies:

Educational policies are strengthened through initiatives such as the strategic dialogue between Kuwait and other countries.

These efforts represent a model of the State of Kuwait's commitment to combating discrimination, thereby enhancing its position in the field of inclusive and quality education and supporting the fourth goal of sustainable development.
