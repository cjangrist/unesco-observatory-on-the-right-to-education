# The Adult Education Act

No. 27, 31 March 2010

## CHAPTER I

### Scope, Aim and Definitions

#### Article 1

##### Scope

This Act covers the organisation of adult education by education and training providers that receive accreditation based on the Act, and public allocation for its implementation.

#### Article 2

##### Objectives

The objectives of adult education according to this Act are:

- to  provide  increased  opportunities  for  active  participation  in  society  to  individuals with short formal education,

- to provide suitable education and training opportunities to individuals on the labour market,  who  have  short  formal  education,  and  enable  them  to  recommence  their studies,

- to provide individuals with a way to increase their vocational skills and enhance their responsibilities in that respect

- to  create  the  necessary  scope  and  solutions  to  meet  the  demands  of  industry  for increased knowledge and competences of employees,

- to provide adult education to individuals with reduced educational and professional opportunities, taking into account their competences and unequal situation,

- to  support  the  recognition  of  the  value  of  education  and  training  that  is  acquired outside of the formal education system and

- to  increase  the  general  educational  level  and  strengthen  the  Icelandic  education system.

#### Article 3

##### Definitions

The definitions of the terms presented in this Act are as follows:

- Adult education : Any kind of study, solution or counselling that is meant to meet the needs of individuals with short formal education and is not based on legislation for upper-secondary education or higher education.

- Recognition  of  non-formal  and  informal  learning :  Organised  process  applied  to evaluate  formally  the  general  knowledge  and  competences  of  an  individual.  The evaluation may be based on the individual's education, work experience, vocational training, leisure courses, adult education, social activities and life experience.

- Education  and  training  provider : Independent  legal  entity  that  provides  adult education and has received accreditation from the Minister of Education, Science and Culture on the basis of this Act.

### Structure of adult education

##### Administration

The Minister of Education, Science and Culture, governs the affairs of adult education according to this Act. This entails responsibility for:

- general policy making for adult education in consultation with stakeholders,

- general administration for the implementation of the Act,

- affairs of the Education and Training Fund,

- support for development and innovation in the field of adult education and

- supervision and evaluation.

#### Article 5

##### Organisation

Adult education shall be focused on the needs of individuals in order to encourage their active participation in society and industry. The organisation of courses offered shall enable individuals to study in parallel with their employment. The confederations of employers and employees shall be consulted in the implementation of this Act and the Minister of Education, Science and Culture may assign tasks related to its implementation to individual institutions or associations cf. Article 15.

Education and training providers that operate on the basis of this Act shall be consulted regularly regarding implementation of adult education. When organising adult education, an effort should be made to ensure its compatibility with other studies and ensure that studies, defined as adult education, can be evaluated for credits within the formal education system. The  Minister of Education, Science and Culture may  issue a Regulation regarding categorisation and levels of adult education based on learning outcomes.

#### Article 6

##### Certification of curricula

Curricula  or  course  descriptions  shall  be  written,  describing  the  objectives  of  adult education, its organisation and content.

The  Minister  of  Education,  Science  and  Culture,  or  a  body  assigned  the  task  by  the Minister  cf.  Article  15,  shall  certify  individual  curricula  and  course  descriptions.  The certification  entails  a  confirmation  that  the  study  fulfils  general  requirements  for  the organisation and quality of teaching, as well as the specific requirements made in each case regarding study content.

#### Article 7

##### Accreditation of education and training providers

The  Minister  of  Education,  Science  and  Culture,  or  a  body  assigned  the  task  by  the Minister, provides accreditation to adult education and training providers. The accreditation shall be based on evaluation of the following:

- teaching and learning facilities,

- organisation and supervision of studies,

- curricula or course descriptions,

- the  competences  of  adult  education  providers,  with  regard  to  their  knowledge  and experience,

- financial issues and insurance, and

- the existence of a quality control system focused on adult education.

The Minister's accreditation cf. paragraph 2 entails a confirmation that the operation of the  relevant  education  and  training  provider  fulfils  the  requirements  of  this  Act  and Regulations thereof, at the time when the accreditation is granted.

The  accreditation  does  not  entail  commitment  for  public  funding  to  the  education provider in question nor responsibility for the education and training provider's liabilities.

Should an education and training provider that has received accreditation no longer fulfil the conditions of this Act and rules and conditions based on it, the Minister may revoke the accreditation.

A Regulation shall further stipulate the requirements for accreditation, its provision and revocation.

The financial accounts of education and training providers shall be signed by a chartered accountant and be accessible to the National Audit no later than three months after closing of the financial year.

#### Article 8

##### Recognition of non-formal and informal learning, guidance and counselling

Individuals with short formal education who choose to embark on studies organised on the basis of this Act or the Upper Secondary School Act are entitled to having their individual non-formal and informal learning evaluated. The evaluation is carried out according to rules set  by  the  Minister  of  Education,  Science  and  Culture  regarding  recognition  of  non-formal and informal learning and its organisation.

Those that intend to embark on studies that fulfil the requirements of this Act are offered individual  guidance and counselling. The  Minister may  issue  rules  regarding  guidance  and counselling cf. this Article.

## CHAPTER III

### Public funding for adult education, Education and Training Fund

#### Article 9

##### Financial contribution

Parliament  (Althingi)  provides  financial  contribution  out  of  the  State  Budget  for  adult education and related activities. There are two kinds of contribution:

- contribution  to  meet  the  costs  of  activities  and  administration  connected  to  adult education in general in accordance with this Act, including regular capital contribution to adult education and training providers and special activities involving education for adults,

- contribution to the Education and Training Fund.

#### Article 10

##### Role of the Education and Training Fund

The role  of  the  Education  and  Training  Fund  is  to  encourage  the  existence  of  suitable educational opportunities for individuals who have short formal education. The Fund should also  participate  in  creating  conditions  that  enable  individuals  to  use  these  educational opportunities. That way, the Fund contributes to fulfilling the aims stipulated in Article 2.

The Education and Training Fund shall administer the financial contribution determined by Parliament (Althingi) in the State Budget at each time. Allocations from the Education and Training  Fund  can  be  divided  into  three  main  categories  according  to  the  allocation  rules confirmed by the Minister of Education, Science and Culture:

- contribution to education and training providers for teaching and organisation of courses,

- contribution  to  cover  the  cost  of  recognition  of  non-formal  and  informal learning,

- grants for innovation and development projects in adult education.

The board of the Education and Training Fund is responsible for the Fund's budget and financial  allocations.  Calls  for  applications  from  education  and  training  providers,  for contribution and support  for teaching and organisation of courses, shall be advertised. The board  of  the  Education  and  Training  Fund  may  give  education  and  training  providers  that already  receive  contributions  form  the  Fund,  a  pact  for  up  to  three-year  contribution,  in proviso of financial contribution from Althingi.

The board of the  Education  and Training  Fund shall  lay  down  special terms  regarding financial contributions to education and training providers and others that receive contributions from the Fund, which shall be confirmed by the Minister of Education, Science and Culture. The Fund shall issue an overview of funded studies.

#### Article 11

##### Board of the Education and Training Fund

The  Minister  of  Education,  Science  and  Culture  appoints  a  nine  person  board  for  the Education  and  Training  Fund  for  a  four-year  term.  The  chairman  is  appointed  without nomination;  the  Confederation  of  Icelandic  Employers  and  the  Icelandic  Confederation  of Labour  shall  nominate  two  representatives  each;  the  Federation  of  State  and  Municipal Employees and the  Association  of  Icelandic  Upper  Secondary  Schools  shall  nominate  one representative  each;  the  Ministry  of  Finance  and  the  Association  of  Local  Authorities  in Iceland shall commonly nominate one representative and the Minister of Social Affairs and Social Security one. Alternates shall be appointed in the same manner.

The board of the Education and Training Fund shall set the operation and allocation rules for the Fund which shall be confirmed by the Minister of Education, Science and Culture.

## CHAPTER IV

### Evaluation and quality control

#### Article 12

##### Objectives

The objectives of evaluation and quality control for adult education are:

- to ensure that education and training providers operate according to provisions of the Act, related rules that may be issued and curricula, cf. Article 6,

- to increase the quality of education and training according to this Act, and

- to ensure that the rights of individuals, that use the services provided on  the basis of this Act, are respected.

#### Article 13

##### Information dissemination and preservation

The Ministry of Education, Science and Culture administers the collection and dissemination  of  information  about  adult  education  with  regard  to  its  policy  making  and supervisory  role.  Education  and  training  providers  that  have  received  accreditation  and support are obliged to provide the Ministry and the board of the Education and Training Fund with information regarding their education and training activities, their results and development  and  any  other  information  deemed  necessary,  such  as  information  about financial provisions and statistics. The Ministry may also make an agreement to assign the tasks stipulated in this Article to another body. In the case of such an arrangement, education and training providers are obliged to provide the requested information to the relevant body.

Education and training providers shall preserve, and provide access to information about studies which their pupils have undertaken and finished.

The  Minister  may  issue  a  Regulation  with  further  provisions  regarding  information obligations  of  education  and  training  providers  cf.  this  Article  and  regarding  treatment  of personal data, including information about pupils' studies.

#### Article 14

##### Quality control implementation

Education and training providers shall carry out a systematic evaluation of the quality and results  of  their  activities  and  publish  information  about  their  internal  quality  control  and subsequent plans for improvement.

The Minister of Education, Science and Culture shall administer surveys, analyses and research that aim at ensuring the general quality of adult education and a general

## CHAPTER V

### Miscellaneous provisions, regulation, entry into force

#### Article 15

##### Administration

The  Ministry  of  Education,  Science  and  Culture  may  make  an  agreement  with  a  legal entity  or  an  institution  to  administer  tasks  specified  in  Article  6  regarding  certification  of curricula; in Article 7 regarding accreditation of education and training providers; in Article 10 regarding administration of the Education and Training Fund; and in Article 13 regarding collection and dissemination of information

#### Article 16

##### Right of appeal

Decisions taken by education and training providers and by the Education and Training Fund cf. this Act may not be appealed to the Minister of Education, Science and Culture.

#### Article 17

##### Regulation

The  Minister  of  Education,  Science  and  Culture  may  issue  a  Regulation  with  further instruction on the implementation of this Act.

#### Article 18

##### Entry into force

This  Act  shall  enter  into  force  on  1  October  2010.  Upon  its  entry  into  force,  the Vocational Training Act no. 19/1992 shall be repealed.
