Free and compulsory education, without any form of discrimination, is the constitutional right of every citizen of Pakistan, as enshrined in the Constitution of Pakistan. The Federal Government, along with the provinces, Azad Jammu and Kashmir, and Gilgit-Baltistan, are legally obligated to uphold this constitutional mandate. All governments in these regions are required to design policies and implement projects that ensure education is accessible to all children and youth, regardless of gender, caste, creed, or community background. In this regard, Articles 25-A, 37-b, and 35 of the Constitution explicitly grant the right to education for all.
