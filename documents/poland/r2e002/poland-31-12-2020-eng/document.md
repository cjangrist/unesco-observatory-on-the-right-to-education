## I.	Legal	instruments	for	the	implementation	of	the	Convention

### I.1.	Ratifications

- Poland has been a party to the Convention against Discrimination in Education since 1964.

- The  ratification  of  the  1962 Protocol  Instituting  a  Conciliation  and  Good  Offices Commission to be Responsible for Seeking the Settlement of Any Disputes which May Arise between States Parties to the Convention against Discrimination in Education is not under consideration.

### I.2.	 The	 protection	 of	 rights	 guaranteed	 in	 the	 Convention	 in	 the	 national legal	system

- The sources of universally binding law of the Republic of Poland shall be: the Constitution, acts, ratified international agreements and regulations (Article 87 of the Constitution). The Constitution shall be the supreme law of the Republic of Poland.

- Pursuant  to  the  constitutional  principle  (Article  32  and  33  of  the  Constitution)  that  no person may be discriminated against in political, social, or economic life for any reason whatsoever, and that women and men shall have equal rights in family, political, social, and economic life, in particular, an equal right to education the education system and the system of higher education in Poland shall provide equal access to education at all stages .

- Article 70 of the Constitution provides that all persons in the Republic of Poland have the right  to  education.  Education  shall  be  compulsory  up  to  the  age  of  18.  The  manner  of pursuing obligatory education and learning duty are defined in the UPO.

- The  terms  and  conditions  of  admission  to  higher  education  institutions,  graduation therefrom and obtaining a diploma are defined in the UPSWiN.

- Education in public schools shall be free of charge. It shall be allowed for higher educational institutions to provide certain educational services for a fee. The terms and conditions for collecting fees and amounts thereof are regulated in

the UPSWiN.

- In  order  to  provide  the  citizens  with  a  universal  and  equal  access  to  education,  public authorities shall create and support systems for individual, financial, and organisational aid for pupils and students. The terms and conditions for granting aid are defined in the Act of 12 March 2004 on Social Welfare (Dziennik Ustaw [ Polish Journal of Laws ] of 2019, item 1507, as amended), the USO and the UPSWiN.

- The Act of 6 January 2005 on National and Ethnic Minorities and Regional Language (Dziennik Ustaw [ Polish Journal of Laws ] of 2017, item 823) - regulates matters connected with  maintaining  and  developing  cultural  identity  of  national  and  ethnic  minorities, preserving and developing regional languages, civic and social integration of members of national and ethnic minorities, as well as the manner of implementing the principle of equal treatment of persons regardless of ethnicity, and determine the tasks and competencies of government administration bodies and local government units relating to these matters.

- Implementation of the right of persons from national and ethnic minorities to learn the language of that minority or to learn in that language, as well as the right of these persons to  learn  history  and  culture  of  that  minority,  as  well  as  implementation  of  the  right  of

## II.	Implementation	of	the	Convention

### II.1.	Access	to	eduaction	-	legal	guarantees

- The  main  legal  basis  of  the  education  system  shall  be  the  Constitution.  In  the  part concerning basic freedoms and rights of citizens, the Constitution stipulates that:

-every person shall have the right to education, education shall be compulsory up to the age of 18,

-education in public schools shall be free of charge, an act may allow for provision of certain education services by public higher education institutions for a fee,

-parents  shall  have  the  right  to  choose  schools  other  than  public  schools  for  their children; citizens and institutions shall have the right to establish schools and higher education institutions,

-public  authorities  shall  provide  the  citizens  with  universal  and  equal  access  to education - for this purpose, they shall create and support individual financial and organisational assistance systems for pupils and students,

-higher education institutions shall have autonomy on terms provided for in an Act.

### II.2. Equal opportunities in education

#### II.2.1. Ensuring the possibility of learning in nursery schools and general schools to all learners

- The commune council shall determine a network of public nursery schools and preschool classes in primary schools operated by the commune. In cases justified by demographic and geographical conditions, the commune council may supplement this network with other  forms  of  pre-school  education  which  are  organised  for  children  at  the  place  of residence or in another location that is as close by as possible.

The network of public and non-public nursery schools and preschool classes in primary schools,  together  with  other  forms  of  pre-school  education,  should  provide  children residing in the commune with the possibility of using pre-school education.

Travel distance of a child performing the obligation of one-year pre-primary education and a five-year-old child from home to a public or non-public nursery school, preschool class in a primary school, another form of preschool education facility where the commune ensures conditions for performing this obligation and exercising the right to preschool education should not exceed 3 km.

-50% of the classes if the learn duty is fulfilled by way of practice at the workplace or attending a qualifying vocational course.

##### Facilitation for persons not being Polish citizens who take external exams

- Pupils or graduates who have limited command of the Polish language that hinders their understanding written text may take:

-an eight-grader exam - except for an eight-grader modern foreign language exam, under conditions and in the form adapted to their educational needs and psychophysical capabilities, based on a positive opinion of the school board;

-maturity exam - except for a modern foreign language, a national minority language, an ethnic  minority language or a regional language maturity exam, under conditions adapted  to  their  needs  and  psychophysical  capabilities,  on  the  basis  of  a  positive opinion of the school board.

Adjustment of the conditions for conducting the eight-grader exam and maturity exam consists, inter alias, in the possibility of the use of a bilingual dictionary, the possibility of sitting the exam in a separate room, appropriate extension of the time allotted for conducting these exams.
