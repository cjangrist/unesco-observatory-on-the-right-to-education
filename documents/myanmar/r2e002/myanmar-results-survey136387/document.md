## 2.1 Early childhood care and education
For reference:
Early childhood care and education (ECCE) is not explicitly provided for in the Convention/Recommendation. However, the international community has increasingly recognized the importance of ECCE over the years. The Committee on the Rights of the Child initially recognized 'the right to education during early childhood as beginning at birth' (General Comment No. 7). This became more explicit with the 2010 Moscow Framework for Action and Cooperation[1] and more recently, the Tashkent Declaration and Commitments to Action for Transforming Early Childhood Care and Education[2].
