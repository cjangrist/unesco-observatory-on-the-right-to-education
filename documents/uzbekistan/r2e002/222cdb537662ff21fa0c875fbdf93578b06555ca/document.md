# The Law of the Republic of Uzbekistan “On Education”

Adopted in 29.08.1997

Extracts  from  the  report  submitted  for  the  7th consultation  of  the  Member  States  on  the implementation of the Convention and Recommendation against Discrimination in Education

## Article 4

The  right  to  receive  education  is  given  to  all,  irrespective  of  sex,  language,  age,  race, nationality,  conscience,  religious  beliefs,  social  origin,  occupation,  social  status,  place  of residence, period of residence in the territory of the Republic of Uzbekistan. Right to education is supplied  by:

- provision for development of government and nongovernmental educational institutions;

- provision of part time and full time training;

- free education based on state programme for personnel training, as well as vocational training based on contract agreements;

- equal  rights  of  graduates  of  all  types  of  the  educational  institutions  while  entering educational institutions of the next grade;

- rights for extern accreditation by educational institutions of citizens educated at home or through self-learning programs.

Citizens of other countries have a right to receive education in the Republic of Uzbekistan according to international agreements.

Persons without citizenship who are permanent residents of Uzbekistan have equal rights to receive education with the citizens of the Republic of Uzbekistan

## Article 18

The  State  shall  support  home  schooling  and  self-education.  Home  schooling  and  self-education  should  be  carried  out  using  the  programmes  of  the  corresponding  educational institutions. Methodical consulting and other help should be given by representatives of state institutions

## Article 20

Students of educational institutions are given the privileges of a scholarship and hostel room according to the normative documents.

## Article 22

The training and support of orphans and children without parents or legal guardians is legally achieved with full state funding.

## Article 23

Specialized  educational  institutions  are  being  organized  to  train  and  treat  physically  and mentally  handicapped  children  and  teenagers.  The  decision  on  children’s  attendance  at educational  institutions  and  removal  from  study  are  taking  by  psychological-medical-pedagogical commission on the basis of agreement with consent of parents or legal guardians.

Tuition fee of students of the specialized educational institutions are fully or partly covered by state funds.

## Article 24

Specialized education institutions are providing educational, professional training and social rehabilitation programs  for  children and  teenagers, who  needs  special  conditions for rehabilitation and learning.

There is special governmental regulation for providing learning and self-learning opportunities for those in prison.
