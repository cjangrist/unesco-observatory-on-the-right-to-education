The government initiated the 'Equal Opportunities Alliance', in which schools, municipalities and civil society partners are working together to improve equal opportunities in education. Within the programme, there is special attention to the wider living environment, like at home, within the neighbourhoud, and public places like libraries. The alliance has an active role in several programmes around equal opportunities, for example the programma 'School and Environment', in which several extra-curricular activities are offered on culture, sports, technique and nature. The government has made a fund available for coalitions and schools to offer these activities. For 2023/2024 and 2024/2025, the budget of the fund is €563.103.423 per year. https://www.gelijke-kansen.nl/

In the Netherlands, there are several legal obligations that prescribes schools and educational institutions to ensure gender equality. For example, there is a 'General law for equal treatment' (Algemene wet gelijke behandeling). Also there are legal requirements for citizenship and social safety, in which gender equality is included.

https://open.overheid.nl/documenten/ronl-84dd2e5a931e5199d817eb7ec40211a688148a09/pdf

The internship pact of vocational education and training (VET) focuses on four themes, one of the themes is eliminating internship discrimination. Within the programme representatives of employers, schools, students, teachers and governement work together on a set of goals and measures to eliminate internship discrimination of VET students. An example of a measure is that VET school must have an easy-to-reach reporting point where students can report internship

discrimination and obtain support and after-care. Or that schools start with objectively matching students with internships in their first year, to prevent discrimination in recruitment and selection of internships.

https://www.government.nl/documents/reports/2023/02/14/vet-internship-pact-2023---2027

https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/onderwerpen/inclusief-onderwijs

The program 'Inclusief onderwijs' aims for an inclusive education system in the Netherlands in 2035. In this school system quality education is provided and all children can develop optimally within their mental and physical capabilities. This approach respects their individual personalities, talents, creativity, and mental and physical abilities. All children are prepared for full and equal participation in society.

What challenges were encountered in achieving gender equality in and through education? If needed, brief quantitative information may be added.

Research by the Education Council shows that differences between boys and girls are not so much due to differences in skills and brains, and that boys do not perform worse due to an overrepresentation of women in primary education. Differences in educational and professional careers arise at home, among peers, at school and in society. They are mainly related to conscious or unconscious ideas about gender. Teachers, pupils and students take these ideas into education. It concerns ideas about what boys and girls are good at and about professions that suit them. This can lead to boys and girls being given unequal opportunities and their freedom of choice being limited.

https://www.onderwijsraad.nl/publicaties/adviezen/2020/10/07/verkenning-sekseverschillen-

onderwijs#:~:text=Verklaring,van%20vrouwen%20in%20het%20basisonderwijs.
