# On National Minorities 1

## Law of the Republic of Belarus

No. 1926-XII of November 11, 1992

[Amended as of May 7, 2007]

The  present  Law  is  based  on  the  Constitution  of  the  Republic  of  Belarus,  principles  of international  law  in  the  sphere  of  human  rights  and  national  minorities  and  is  aimed  at creation  of  conditions  for  free  development  of  national  minorities  as  well  as  protection  of rights and legal interests of persons who refer themselves to national minorities.

### Article 1.
For the purposes of the present Law persons belonging to national minorities are persons  permanently  residing  on  the  territory  of  the  Republic  of  Belarus  and  having citizenship  of  the  Republic  of  Belarus  whose  origin,  language,  culture  or  traditions  are different from those of main population of the republic.

### Article 2.
Belonging to national minority is a matter of personal choice of a citizen of the Republic of Belarus.

Choice of the citizen of the Republic of Belarus of belonging to national minority does not cause any unfavorable consequences.

### Article 3.
Citizens of the Republic of Belarus referring themselves to national minorities shall observe the Constitution of the Republic of Belarus and other acts of legislation of the Republic of Belarus, assist to preservation of sovereignty and territorial unity of the Republic of Belarus, respect traditions of the citizens of all nationalities that populate the republic, their language and culture.

### Article  4.
Any  direct  or  indirect  limitation  of  rights  and  freedoms  of  citizens  of  the Republic of Belarus because of their belonging to national  minority as well as attempts of assimilation against their will are prohibited.

### Article 5.
Nobody can be forced to determine and indicate his (her) national belonging as well as to prove the national belonging or refuse from it.

### Article 6.
A state guarantees the citizens of the Republic of Belarus referring themselves to  national  minorities  to  have  equal  political,  economic  and  social  rights  and  freedoms enjoyed in order established by legislation of the Republic of Belarus in particular:

right to receive assistance from state for developing national culture and education;

### Article  7.
Consulting bodies from representatives of national minorities may be created and  operate  on  public  basis  at  local  Councils  of  Deputies.  Procedure  of  their  creation  is determined by relevant local Councils of Deputies.

### Article 8.
The state in order established by legislation of the Republic of Belarus assists to creating the conditions for development of education and cultures of national minorities by giving necessary money assets from republican and local budgets.

### Article 9.
Public associations of citizens referring themselves to national minorities have the right to carry out entrepreneurial activity in accordance with legislation of the Republic of Belarus.

### Article 10.
Public associations of citizens referring themselves to national minorities may create cultural and enlightenment institutions.

Training  of  specialists  for  providing  interests  of  national  minorities  in  the  sphere  of education  and  culture  is  carried  out  by  educational  institutions  of  the  Republic  of  Belarus including training on contractual basis concluded with public associations of citizens referring themselves to national minorities.

### Article 11.
Historical and cultural values of national minorities are a part of culture of the Republic of Belarus and are protected by state in accordance with legislation of the Republic of Belarus.

### Article 12.
Creation and activity of public associations of citizens referring themselves to national minorities is prohibited if it contradicts to legislation of the Republic of Belarus on public  associations  as  well  as  if  the  mentioned  public  associations  are  organizationally connected or are a part of political organization of a foreign state.

### Article 13.
Citizens of the Republic of Belarus regardless of their national belonging enjoy equal protection of the state.

Any  actions  aimed  at  discrimination  based  on  nationality,  creation  of  obstacles  in realization of their rights by national minorities, stimulation of hostility among nationalities entail responsibility in accordance with legislation of the Republic of Belarus.
