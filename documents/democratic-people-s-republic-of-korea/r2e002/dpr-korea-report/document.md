# Report On Implementation Of Recommendations Against Discrimination In Education

Education Commission Democratic People's Republic of Korea 2020

## Forward

The  Democratic  People's  Republic  of  Korea  (DPRK)  has  established  the well-regulated pre-school system, formal secondary and higher education system, and the informal in-service education and social education system, under which all people in the country enjoy free education.

Upholding the slogan of making people well-versed in science and technology,  the  DPRK  Government  takes  several  measures  to  ensure  that  all people have free access to life-long education to their hearts' content.

In  line  with  the  demand  of  times  the  DPRK  Government  makes  positive efforts to put education on IT and modern basis as early as possible, to improve the content and methods of education, to upgrade the conditions and environment of education, and to bring about a fresh turn in secondary general and other levels of education.

In addition, it works hard to raise its level of education to the advanced world level as soon as possible in closer cooperation with UNESCO, UNICEF and other international organizations.

The  1960  Convention  and  Recommendations  against  Discrimination  in Education which underlines the important global norms for the right to education reflect  the  mission  of  UNESCO  in  its  Charter  to  promote  the  ideal  of  equitable access  to  education  without  discrimination  based  on  racial,  gender  and  other economic and social differences.

Education  2030  Agenda  set  the  SDG  of  ensuring  inclusive  and  equitable quality  education  and  promoting  lifelong  learning  opportunities  for  all  and  its related objectives.

Since  the  adoption  of  Education  2030  Agenda,  the  DPRK  has  drawn nation-wide public attention to dynamically step up the effort to ensure inclusive and equitable quality education and promote lifelong learning opportunities for all and its related objectives This  report  includes  the  achievements  and  experience  gained  by  DPRK  in implementing the goal of Education 2030 Agenda in the period of 2017 to 2020, in conformity  with  Article  8  of  UNESCO  Charter  which  requires  that  each  of  its member states presents its regular report on the measures taken by it to implement the conventions and recommendation adopted by UNESCO.

## 1. Legal Foundation for Educational Development

Implementation of the education policies pursued by the DPRK Government is firmly ensured by the Socialist Constitution of the DPRK, the Law of the DPRK on Education and other laws for different sectors.

The Socialist Constitution of the DPRK was adopted at the First Session of the Fifth Supreme People's Assembly on Dec. 27, 1972 and subsequently it was revised and supplemented on nine occasions to meet the requirement of the times in its development.

The DPRK adopted the Law on Nursing and Upbringing of Children at the Sixth Session of the Fifth Supreme People's Assembly on April 29, 1976. Then, it adopted the Law of the DPRK on Education, the Law of the DPRK on General Education, the Law of the DPRK on Higher Education, the Law of the DPRK on Teachers, the Law of the DPRK on Technical and Vocational Education and the Law of the DPRK on the Implementation of Education Programme by decrees of the Presidium of the Supreme People's Assembly. It also adopted the Law of the DPRK on Distance Education as a decree of the Supreme People's Assembly.

The Sixth Session of the twelfth Supreme People's Assembly held on Sept. 25, 2012  adopted the decree of the  Supreme  People's  Assembly  On  the Introduction of Universal 12-year Compulsory Education.

The Socialist Constitution, the specific laws for education and the decrees of the DPRK are the concentrated reflection of the education policies of the state.

The DPRK has stipulated by law that all its citizens have the obligation for compulsory secondary general education and the right to free education.

The obligation of the entire population for secondary general education and the right to free education constitute the most basic guarantee for the implementation of  the  Education  2030  Agenda  targeting  inclusive  and  equitable education.

Article  45  of  the  Socialist  Constitution  of  the  DPRK  stipulates,  'The  state shall develop  the universal 12-year compulsory  education  including  1-year pre-school education at high level to meet the trend of the development of modern science and technology and the practical requirements of socialist construction', and  Article  47  reads,  'The  state  shall  provide  all  students  with  free  access  to education and give scholarship to students of universities and specialized schools '.

The  Law  of  the  DPRK  on  Education  adopted  by  Decree  No.  847  of  the Presidium of the Supremme People's Assembly on July 14, 1999 and revised and supplemented on Dec. 23, 2015 reads: All DPRK citizens shall have the obligation for  secondary  general  education  and  the  right  to  free  education.  All  forms  of education is free in the DPRK. Any educational establishment shall be prohibited to receive any fee for admission, lessons, internship, study tour and excursion from any student, his or her parents, or his or her guardian.

The DPRK defined by law that special attention shall be paid to the provision of technical and vocational education to all its citizens according to their hopes and aptitudes, as well as to women and people with disabilities.

Creation of all necessary conditions for all citizens, particularly women and persons  with  diabilities,  to  have  access  to  technical  and  vocational  education according  to  their  hopes  and  aptitudes  is  one  of  the  important  factors  for  the implementation  of  the  1960  Convention  and  Recommendations  on  equitable access  to  education  without  discrimination  based  on  racial,  gender  and  other economic and social differences.

The Law of the DPRK on Technical and Vocational Education adopted by Decree No. 2315 of the Presidium of the Supreme People's Assembly on July 12, 2018 stipulates that the state shall create all necessary conditions for all its citizens to have access to vocational and technical education according to their hopes and aptitudes and pay special attention to that for women and people with diabilities.

With  the  adoption  of  the  Law  of  the  DPRK  on  Technical  and  Vocational Education, the DPRK has provided a firm legal guarantee for the access of all its citizens to vocational and technical education.

The  DPRK  has  made  it  law  to  promote  social  education  and  provide  all necessary conditions for learning to all its working people.

Promotion of social education is an important way to implement the SDG for the creation of opportunity for lifelong education for all.

The Socialist Constitution of the DPRK reads: The state shall promote social education  and  provide  all  its  working  people  with  all  necessary  conditions  for learning.

The Law of the DPRK on Distance Education adopted by the Presidium of the Supreme People's Assembly on April 12, 2020 includes the provision that the well-regulated  distance  education  system  and  order  shall  be  established  for  all members of society to have access to higher education while working with a view to  contributing  to  making  all  people  well-versed  in  science  and  technology  and turning the country into a power of talents.

The Law  of the DPRK  on  Distance Education defines its mission, establishment of distance education system, enrollment of students, formulation of the distance education programme and its implementation, provision of conditions for it and all other issues related to distance education.

The  DPRK  has  adopted  a  law  on  providing  in  a  responsible  way  the conditions for education and life of parentless children or those without guardians, the  children  with  disabilities  in  seeing  and  hearing  and  other  disabilities  at  the expense of the state.

The Law of the DPRK on General Education adopted by the Decision No. 1355 of the Presidium of the Supreme People's Assembly on Jan. 19, 2011 and revised  and  supplemented  on  Dec.  23,  2015  provides  that  the  state  shall  be responsible  for  the  conditions  of  education  and  living  for  parentless  children  or those without guardians, children with disabilities in seeing, hearing and speaking and other disabilities.

Since  the  first  day  of  its  founding,  DPRK  has  taken  legal  and  institutional measures for preferential treatment and protection of persons with disabilities at the expense of the state.

The Law of the DPRK on Protection of Persons with Disabilities adopted by Decree No. 3835 of the Presidium of the Supreme People's Assembly on June 18, 2003 and amended on Nov. 21, 2013 provides the fundamentals for education of the persons with disabilities, the registration and care of pre-school children with disabilities,  their  enrollment,  their  right  to  higher  education,  organization  and operation of special schools for them, formulation of educational programme for them,  publication  of  special  educational  materials  for  them,  organization  and management of vocational schools for them, and development of the braille and sign  language.  It  also  includes  the  provisions  to  form  special  classes  in  general schools  or  establish  special  schools  like  schools  for  persons  with  disabilities  in hearing  and  seeing  and  mental  cultivation  schools  according  to  their  physical mental characteristics  and  types  of  disabilities.  It  also  stipulates  that  students  of special schools shall be given scholarship.

With the  adoption  of  the  Law  of  the  DPRK  on  General  Education  and  the Law of the DPRK on Protection of Persons with Disabilities, the DPRK has laid a firm legal foundation for parentless children or those without guardians, children with disabilities in seeing, hearing and speaking and other disabilities to have the same  obligation  and  access  to  education  as  normal  children  and  make  positive contributions to the development of society.

The DPRK acceded to the 'Marrakesh Treaty to facilitate access to publish works for persons who are blind, visually impaired, or otherwise print disabled' on May 19, 2016.

## 2. Improved Qualification of Teaching Staff

For  the  implementation  of  the  SDG  for  equitable  access  to  inclusive  and equitable  education  of  high  quality  envisaged  in  Education  2030  Agenda,  it  is essential to improve the qualification of teaching staff who are directly responsible for it.

[[END OF PAGE 7]]

[[START OF PAGE 8]]

With the view that bringing about a radical turn in education is immediately improving the qualification of teaching staff, the DPRK Government has directed great efforts to the latter and attached nation-wide, social importance to it.

It has ensured that all universities of education and teachers training colleges take  measures  to  update  their  teaching  programme  and  improve  the  quality  of education in an innovative way to produce highly qualified teachers.

It  also  ensured  that  the  primary  and  secondary  education  sectors  enroll  all school  teachers,  including  those  of  rural  and  mountainous  areas,  in  refreshment courses and distance education system to improve their qualification to meet the requirement of the development of education.

The state has channeled great efforts to narrowing the gap between urban and rural areas in secondary education.

Since  2018,  it  has  established  the  system,  under  which  the  graduates  of universities of education and teachers training colleges are engaged in teaching at schools  in  rural  and  mountainous  areas.  Meanwhile,  it  has  encouraged  them  to volunteer to them in order to consolidate the teaching staff there. From 2017 to 2020 more than 800 graduates of the teachers training institutions volunteered to the branch schools in remote mountainous areas and solitary islets.

For  the  improvement  of  education  for  children  with  disabilities  in  seeing, hearing and speaking and other disabilities, it is important to train teachers capable for  special  education.  It  is  not  only  aimed  at  improving  the  quality  of  special education, but also at ensuring that all the people with disabilities make positive contribution to social development with the rights equal to other people.

The DPRK Government now works hard to the qualification of teachers at schools for children with disabilities in seeing, hearing and speaking, for example, it has set up the center for training teachers of their schools.

## 3. Perfection of Education System

The DPRK Government ensures that the secondary general education system is improved and consolidated in line with the requirement of social development.

DPRK has introduced the universal 12-year compulsory education since April 1, 2014. This system provides compulsory and free secondary general education to all children and students of the 5~17 age group throughout the country..

The DPRK has set up technical senior middle schools in the metallurgical, coal-mining, chemical, electrical, fishing, agricultural, fruit farming and stockbreeding  fields,  and  since  April  1,  2017,  has  ensured  that  senior  middle school students receive basic technical education which suits the economical and geographical features of the regions in which they live.

In 2018 it established 11 IT senior middle schools on an experimental basis, and in 2020, with the experience gained from their operation, it formed IT classes at 195 senior middle schools across the country.

The DPRK Government has improved and consolidated the special education system, under which all the people with disabilities acquire the knowledge, skills and arts so that they can contribute to the state and social development with the obligations and rights equal to other people.

With the  increase  of  universal  compulsory  education  course  from  11  to  12 years, all the schools for children with disabilities in seeing, hearing and speaking have  also  began  to  implement  the  12-  year  programme  like  other  schools  since April 1, 2016.

The  DPRK  Government  saw  to  it  that  the  number  of  higher  education institutions be increased to meet the requirement of the effort to make all people well-versed in science and technology.

Since  2017  the  number  of  factory,  farm  and  fishermen's  colleges  has increased, and  tens of technical and  vocational  colleges  have  been  newly established more.

The  DPRK  Government  has  also  taken  measure  to  expand  the  distance education  courses  and  improve  their  quality,  and  thus  hundreds  of  thousands working people are now involved in the distance education system to acquire the scientific and technical knowledge conducive to their own production activities.

## 4. Innovation of Teaching Content and Methods

For inclusive and equitable quality education, the SDG included in Education 2030 Agenda, it is imperative to constantly innovate teaching content and methods as required by the requirement of the times.

The DPRK Government directs great efforts to formulating and implementing the universal 12-year compulsory education programme of higher standard.

It has made comprehensive analysis of the issues arising in the implementation of the universal 12-year compulsory education programme, and on that  basis,  actively  encouraged  schools  to  make  education  practical  and  modern, increase the proportion of practice and improve the teaching content and methods in a proactive way.

It has ensured that technical senior middle schools formulate and implement a new  education  programme  so  that  their  students  can  get  different  forms  of practical education suited to the economic and geographical features of their own regions.

It  also  defined  the  new  teaching  content  of  the  schools  for  children  with disabilities  in  seeing,  hearing  and  speaking  in  2016.  In  particular,  it  has  ensured that they update the content of IT and basic technical education as required by the developing reality so that their students can get jobs in IT and other sectors after graduation and make their own service to social development.

Improvement of teaching mehtods is essential for giving quality education to all students.

The  DPRK  Government  has  paid  special  attention  to  improvement  of teaching methods at schools in the rural and mountainous areas.

It has tried hard to assist in different forms and ways the teachers in the rural and mountainous areas in their preparation for lessons and improvement of their qualification: in 2017 it sent over 6, 600 teachers in the urban areas to the schools in  the  rural  and  mountainous  areas  to  show  them  the  demonstration  lessons  for about 45,000 hours and organized for more than 6,900 teachers in the rural and mountainous  ares  the  observation  of  lessons  given  by  the  excellent  teachers  in urban areas. It has also made positive contributions to the improvement of teaching methods  by  providing  educational  multimedia  and  video  materials  of  model lessons to them on a regular basis.

## 5. Improvement of Educational Conditions and Environment

The DPRK Government has made energetic efforts to raise the material and technological  foundation  of  education  onto  the  world  standard  by  upgrading  the educational conditions and environment.

It has continued to pay deep attention and channel great efforts to providing better educational environment to all its citizens.

Improvement  of  educational  environment  as  required  by  the  century  is  to create  material  preconditions  for  inclusive  and  equitable  quality  education,  the SDG included in Education 2030 Agenda.

Above all, the DPRK Government has made dynamic efforts to provide better environment for social education to all its citizens.

In January 2016, it built, as a model of social education center, the Sci-Tech Complex,  the  palace  of  education  for  all  and  the  multi-functional  scientific  and technological  knowledge  extension  center.  Then,  all  establishments,  enterprises, factories and cooperative farms have linked their own scientific and technological knowledge extension rooms on-line to the Sci-Tech Complex and operated them on a regular basis so that all working people update scientific and technological knowledge  while  working  and  make  positive  contributions  to  the  technological development of their own units.

Now the Sci-Tech Complex is open to students ranging from primary schools to  universities,  teachers,  researchers  and  all  other  citizens.  They  also  acquire modern scientific and technological knowledge on-line.

It  has  rebuilt  the  children's  camps  and  schoolchildren's  palaces  in  a  modern way  to  provide  better,  pleasant  environment  for  camping  life  and  after-school activities to all children.

And since 2017, according to the year-to-year plan, it has newly built or rebuilt schools  of  general  education  in  a  modern  way  and  turned  all  classrooms  into multifunctional ones.

In  particular,  it  has  made  full  investigation  into  the  conditions  of  school buildings  in  the  rural  and  mountainous  areas  and  provided  necessary  labour, materials  and  fund  preferentially  to  them.  Accordingly,  since  2017  hundreds  of schools  have  been  newly  built  or  modernized  every  year.  And  all  schools  are connected to the national network so that they can share educational resources.

It  built,  as  models of teachers training centers, Pyongyang Teachers Training College in 2017 and Kanggye TeachersvTraining College in 2019. With them as an  example,  each  of  the  provinces  is  now  stepping  up  the  construction  of  its teachers training college at the final stage.

## Conclusion

This report is made on the basis of the achievements and experiences gained by  the  DPRK  Government  from  2017  to  2020  in  its  effort  to  attain  the  goal  of 'ensure inclusive and equitable quality education and promote life-long learning opportunities for all' included in Education 2030 Agenda.

For the last 4 years DPRK has channeled primary efforts to improvement of teachers'  qualification  and  actively  promoted  perfection  of  education  system, innovation of teaching content and methods  and  upgrading  of  educational conditions and environment.

In  spite  of  the  great  successes  it  has  made  during  the  period  under  review, there still exists considerable gap between schools in rural and urban areas in terms of education quality and conditions and it still has several difficulties in creating full  education  conditions  and  environment  for  the  schools  of  the  children  with disabilities  in  seeing,  hearing  and  speaking  and  other  disabilities  and  classes  for groups of the children with disabilities.

At  present  DPRK  is  faced  with  the  heavy  task  to  thoroughly  implement  the new National Education Development Strategy and its high objectives.

As in the past so also in future, the DPRK Government will further develop education  to  meet  the  latest  trend  of  education  development  and  pedagogical requirements  and  raise  its  education  to  the  world  advanced  level  as  soon  as possible by making dynamic efforts to attain the goal of Education 2030 Agenda to ensure  inclusive  and  equitable  access  to  quality  education  and  create  more opportunities  for  lifelong  learning  for  all  in  close  cooperation  with  UNESCO, UNICEF and other international organizations.
