# BOUGAINVILLE EDUCATION ACT 2013

Being an Act to provide for the establishment and implementation of the Bougainville Education System, and for related purposes.

MADE by the House of Representatives to come into operation in accordance with a notice in the *Bougainville Gazette* by the Minister.

## PART 1 PRELIMINARY

#### 1. OBJECTS OF THIS ACT.

The objects of this Act are:

- (a) to establish and implement the Bougainville Education System; and

- (b) to give effect to section 32 of the *Bougainville Constitution*; and

- (c) to regulate the administration of education services in Bougainville; and

- (d) to promote integrity and appropriate standards in education.

- (b) the right to freedom of conscience, thought and religion conferred by paragraph 180(1)(c) of the *Bougainville Constitution*; and

- (c) the right to freedom of expression conferred by paragraph 180(1)(d) of the *Bougainville Constitution*; and

#### 4. COMPULSORY ENROLMENT.

(1) Each parent of a child who is of compulsory school age must ensure that the child is enrolled at a school, unless the parent has a reasonable excuse:

Penalty: In the case of:

- (a) a first offence of K100; or

- (b) a subsequent offence of K50, whether or not relating to the same child of the parent.

(2) Without limiting subsection (1), it is a reasonable excuse for a parent (the relevant parent) that:

- (a) the child lives with another parent and the relevant parent believes, on reasonable grounds, that the other parent is complying with subsection (1); or

- (b) in all the circumstances, the relevant parent is not reasonably able to control the child's behaviour to the extent necessary to comply with subsection (1).

(3) Proceedings for an offence against subsection (1) shall be brought against a parent only with the Secretary's written approval.

(4) Subsection (1) does not apply to a parent of:

- (a) a child approved by the Secretary for home education; or

- (b) a child in respect of whom an exemption has been granted by the Secretary.

(5) The Secretary may, on application in writing by the parent of a child, approve the child for home education if the Secretary is satisfied that the proposed home education of the child meets the prescribed criteria.

(6) The Secretary may, on application in writing by the parent of a child, issue a certificate of exemption in respect of the child if the Secretary is satisfied that the exemption:

[[END OF PAGE 11]]

[[START OF PAGE 12]]

- (a) is necessary or desirable, having regard to an educational, vocational training or employment opportunity offered to the child; or

- (b) is otherwise in the child's best interests, having regard to the care, health, development or education of the child.

(7) The Secretary may issue the certificate of exemption subject to written conditions.

(8) The Secretary may cancel a certificate of exemption if:

- (a) the certificate was issued in error; or

- (b) the reason for which the certificate was issued ceases to apply.

(9) To avoid doubt, if:

- (a) a child turns 6 years of age on or before 1 June in a particular year, the child must be enrolled to commence school at the start of that year; and

- (b) a child turns 6 years of age after 1 June in a particular year, the child must be enrolled to commence school at the start of the following year.

#### 5. COMPULSORY ATTENDANCE.

(1) Each parent of a child who is of compulsory school age must ensure that the child attends a school during the whole of each school day, unless the parent has a reasonable excuse:

Penalty: In the case of:

- (a) a first offence of K100; or

- (b) a subsequent offence of K50, whether or not relating to the same child of the parent.

(2) Without limiting subsection (1), it is a reasonable excuse for a parent (the relevant parent) that:

- (a) the child lives with another parent and the relevant parent believes, on reasonable grounds, that the other parent is complying with subsection (1); or

- (b) in all the circumstances, the relevant parent is not reasonably able to control the child's behaviour to the extent necessary to comply with subsection (1).

(3) Subsection (1) does not apply to the parent of a child if:

- (a) the child is suspended or has been expelled from the school; or

- (b) the child has been granted an exemption under section 4; or

#### 6. BOUGAINVILLE EDUCATION SYSTEM.

(1) The Bougainville Education System is established.

(2) The Bougainville Education System consists of the following schools, colleges, centres and institutions that are conducted within the Autonomous Region of Bougainville by Education Agencies:

- (a) pre-schools;

- (b) vernacular schools;

- (c) elementary schools;

- (d) primary schools;

- (e) technical schools;

- (f) secondary schools;

- (g) distance education schools;

- (h) technical secondary schools;

- (i) inclusive education centres;

- (j) community colleges;

- (k) adult education centres;

- (1) tertiary institutions.

(3) The Bougainville Education System is to be administered in accordance with, and for the objects and purposes expressed in, the *Bougainville Constitution* and this Act.

#### 7. OBJECTS OF THE BOUGAINVILLE EDUCATION SYSTEM.

(1) The objects of the Bougainville Educational System are:

- (a) to strive to achieve universal primary, secondary and adult education; and

- (b) to provide, from elementary and primary levels, the widest possible range of technical education relevant to the needs of Bougainville; and

- (၁) to provide for appropriate levels of tertiary education; and

- (d) to ensure that all children, throughout the primary and secondary levels of education, are taught to read and write in their vernacular and are taught about their own culture; and

- (e) to provide the highest possible standard of education to enable all students to participate fully in the lives of the Bougainville communities to which they belong; and

- (f) to ensure that all Education Authorities work in partnership.

(2) The Minister must consult regularly with the Councils of Elders, traditional chiefs, other traditional leaders and other community leaders about the development and effectiveness of the curriculum in all levels and kinds of education within the Bougainville Education System.

- (a) the Bougainville Executive Council;

- (b) the Minister;

- (c) the Secretary;

- (d) the Teaching Service Commission;

- (e) the Bougainville Education Board;

- (f) the Education Agencies;

- (g) the District Education Officers;

#### 9. FUNCTIONS OF THE BOUGAINVILLE EXECUTIVE COUNCIL.

(1) The functions of the Bougainville Executive Council in relation to education matters are:

- (a) to give directions to, and receive recommendations from, the Bougainville Education Board on any matter relating to the Bougainville Education System; and

- (b) to recognize Education Agencies; and

- (c) to recognize schools and institutions as members of the Bougainville Education System; and

- (d) to perform such other functions as are conferred on the Council by or under this Act or any other law.

(2) The Bougainville Executive Council may, by written instrument, delegate all or any of its powers or functions under this Act, other than this power of delegation, to the Minister.

#### 12. FUNCTIONS OF THE DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION.

(1) The Department through the Secretary is the Executive Office of the Bougainville Education System.

(2) The Department:

- (a) is the Education Agency for the schools and institutions conducted by the Autonomous Bougainville Government; and

- (b) is responsible for the carrying out and supervision of the functions and responsibilities of the Autonomous Bougainville Government in the Bougainville Education System; and

- (c) is to provide teachers, teaching materials, text books, libraries and such other educational resources and services as it considers necessary and desirable to maintain and improve the Bougainville Education System; and

- (d) is to co-ordinate the operations of all Education Agencies within the Bougainville Education System.

#### 13. BOUGAINVILLE EDUCATION PLAN.

(1) After consultation with Education Agencies, Councils of Elders, non-government organizations and other education stakeholders, the Bougainville Education Board must recommend to the Bougainville Executive Council a Bougainville Education Plan.

(2) The Bougainville Education Plan must provide for the establishment, conduct, maintenance and development of schools and institutions and other education facilities and services in Bougainville.

(3) The Bougainville Executive Council may approve the Bougainville Education Plan, with or without amendments.

(4) The Bougainville Executive Council may approve other plans and policies relating to education in Bougainville recommended by the Bougainville Education Board to support the Bougainville Education Plan.

(5) The Bougainville Education Board must review the Bougainville Education Plan at least every 5 years.

#### 14. COMPLIANCE WITH BOUGAINVILLE EDUCATION PLAN.

(1) An Education Agency must not:

- (a) establish schools or institutions; or

- (b) conduct schools or institutions; or

- (c) provide other education facilities or services;

unless the schools or institutions, or other education facilities or services, are consistent with the Bougainville Education Plan.

(2) If the Bougainville Executive Council is satisfied that an Education Agency has not complied with subsection (1) in relation to a school or institution, or other education facility or service, the Bougainville Executive Council may do all or any of the following:

- (a) not approve the school or institution, or other education facility or service, to be part of the Bougainville Education System;

- (b) remove the school or institution, or other education facility or service, from the Bougainville Education System;

- (၁) withdraw approval for payment of any financial or other benefits accruing in respect of the school or institution, or other education facility or service.

(3) The Bougainville Executive Council may revoke or vary a decision made under subsection (2) if the Council is satisfied that an Education Agency has brought the school or institution, or other education facility or service, into consistency with the Bougainville Education Plan.

#### 29. RECOGNITION OF EDUCTION AGENCIES.

## PART 6 MEMBER AND PERMITTED SCHOOLS AND INSTITUTIONS.

#### 45. MEMBER SCHOOLS AND INSTITUTIONS.

(1) The Bougainville Executive Council may, on the recommendation of the Bougainville Education Board, recognise a school or institution as a member school or member institution of the Bougainville Education System if the Council is satisfied that:

- (a) the school or institution will admit students irrespective of religious or doctrinal affiliations and that it will generally be conducted in accordance with the objects of the Bougainville Education System and this Act; and

- (b) only suitably qualified teachers will be engaged in teaching the approved curricula at the school or institution, other than pre-schools and vernacular schools; and

- (c) teaching at the school or institution will conform to the approved curricula, and appropriate arrangements and undertakings have been made accordingly.

(2) The Bougainville Executive Council may, on the recommendation of the Bougainville Education Board, cancel the recognition of a member school or member institution if the Council is satisfied that:

- (a) the school or institution has ceased to comply with a recognition requirement under subsection (1); or

- (b) the arrangements and undertakings required for recognition of the school or institution have not been continued in force or complied with.

(3) Before deciding whether or not to cancel the recognition of a member school or member institution under subsection (2), the Bougainville Executive Council must give the Education Agency conducting the school or institution full opportunity of making representations and of being heard in the matter.

(4) On the written request of the Education Agency conducting a member school or member institution, the Bougainville Executive Council may cancel recognition of the school or institution.

(5) The Bougainville Executive Council must give the Education Agency concerned written notice of any decision it makes under this section.

#### 46. PERMITTED SCHOOLS AND INSTITUTIONS.

(1) A permitted school or permitted institution is not within the Bougainville Education System.

(2) A person or organization must not conduct a permitted school or permitted institution unless the school or institution is registered.

Penalty: A fine not exceeding K10, 000.00

(3) A person or organization wishing to conduct a permitted school or permitted institution must apply in the prescribed form to the Secretary for registration of the school or institution.

(4) On receipt of an application for registration, the Secretary must consider the application and may, having regard to the advice of the Bougainville Education Board, grant or refuse to grant a certificate of registration.

(5) Regulations may provide for matters concerning the registration of permitted schools and permitted institutions.

(9) The Bougainville Executive Council must give the person or organization wishing to conduct, or conducting, a permitted school or institution written notice of any decision it makes under this section.

#### 47. REGISTER OF MEMBER AND PERMITTED SCHOOLS AND INSTITUTIONS.

(1) The Department must establish and maintain a register of:

- (a) member schools and member institutions; and

- (b) permitted schools and permitted institutions.

(2) The register is to contain such information and be in such form as the Secretary determines.

## PART 7 ADMINISTRATION GENERALLY.

#### 49. CLOSURE OF SCHOOLS AND INSTITUTIONS.

	(1) The Bougainville Executive Council may, on the recommendation of the Bougainville Education Board, order the closure of a school or institution if it is satisfied that:

- (a) the standard or type of education, or curriculum or training (other than religious or doctrinal education or training) at the school or institution is not meeting the prescribed standards; or

- (b) following the suspension of teaching at the school or institution under section 57, the closure of the school or institution is necessary having regard to the existing circumstances.

(2) Without limiting subsection (1), the Bougainville Executive Council may:

- (a) in the case of a member school or member institution, order the closure of the school or institution if the recognition of the school or institution is cancelled under section 45; or

- (b) in the case of a permitted school or permitted institution, order the closure of the school or institution if the registration of the school or institution is cancelled under section 46.

(3) Before deciding whether to order the closure of a school or institution, the Bougainville Executive Council must give the Education Agency or person conducting the school or institution full opportunity of making representations and of being heard in the matter.

(4) A person who conducts or is associated with the conducting of a school or institution that has been closed is guilty of an offence.

Penalty: A fine not exceeding K10,000.00

(5) A person who teaches at a school or institution that has been closed is guilty of an offence.

Penalty: A fine not exceeding K5,000.00

(6) If the Bougainville Executive Council orders the closure of a school or institution:

- (a) the teaching positions at the school or institution are deemed to be abolished, and the persons occupying those positions are deemed to be appointed to non- institutional positions created under the *PNG Teaching Service Act*; and

- (b) the chairperson of the Education Authority of the school or institution must within 14 days provide to the Teaching Service Commission a written report setting our the reasons for the order.

(7) The Bougainville Executive Council must give the Education Agency concerned, or the person or organization conducting the permitted school or permitted institution concerned, written notice of any decision the Council makes under this section.

(8) To avoid doubt, this section applies to member schools, member institutions, permitted schools and permitted institutions.

#### 50. LIMITATION OF FUNCTIONS IN RELATION TO TEACHERS.

(1) Except as is specifically provided for by or under this Act or any other law relating to education matters, an Education Authority, other than the Teaching Service Commission, does not have any powers in relation to the appointment, promotion, transfer, discipline, suspension, dismissal or conditions of service of teachers generally or of individual teachers in the Teaching Service.

(2) Nothing in subsection (1) prevents an Education Agency or Governing Board:

- (a) from making out of its own resources:

  - (i) payments of any kind to additional teachers appointed by the Governing Board; or

  - (ii) with the approval of the Teaching Service Commission, payments to all or any members of the Teaching Service of additional benefits including overseas and attraction allowances; or

- (b) from reporting in writing to the Bougainville Education Board any matter relating to the appointment and discipline of members of the Teaching Service; or

- (c) otherwise making provision for all or any members of the Teaching Service or additional teachers at a school or institution.

#### 51. FEES AND ALLOWANCES.

(1) Subject to subsection (2), the members and alternate members of the Bougainville Education Board or a Governing Board are to be paid such fees, allowances and expenses as are prescribed.

(2) Members or alternate members of the Bougainville Education Board or a Governing Board who are officers or employees of the Autonomous Bougainville Government or members of the Teaching Service are not to be paid any fees, allowances or expenses.

#### 52. INSPECTIONS.

- (b) bring charges before the Teaching Service Commission.

(2) Nothing in subsection (1) authorises the inspection of teachers.

#### 53. PARENTS', CITIZENS', AND FRIENDS' ASSOCIATIONS.

	(1) An Education Agency conducting a school or institution may approve an association to be the Parents', Citizens' and Friends' Association for the school or institution.

(2) The Parents', Citizens' and Friends' Association for a school or institution:

- (a) is to represent parents and friends of students, and other persons interested in the education and welfare of students, attending the school or institution; and

- (b) must have a constitution approved by the Education Agency conducting the school or institution.

(3) The Parents', Citizens' and Friends' Association for a school or institution may, by arrangement with the Governing Board of the school or institution, use the buildings, grounds and facilities of the school or institution for holding fetes and other activities for the benefit of the school or institution.

(4) A Parents', Citizens' and Friends Association must ensure that:

- (a) fetes and other activities held by it do not disrupt or interfere with teaching at the school or institution; and

- (b) any money raised for the benefit of the school or institution is properly accounted for to the satisfaction of the Education Agency conducting the school or institution.

(5) The activities not permitted as part of the fund raising are:

- (a) the sale of alcoholic refreshments, or other illicit items on school or institution premises; and

- (b) gambling, lotteries, raffles or other games of chance other than those licensed or permitted by law; and

- (c) the provision of students as laborers to the general public.

(7) The Bougainville Education Board or an Education Agency conducting a school or institution may direct audit inspections to be carried out on the accounts kept by the Parents', Citizens' and Friends' Association of the school or institution for the Association's fund raising activities.

#### 54. GOVERNING BOARDS MAY RAISE AND EXPEND MONEY.

	(1) Subject to the approval of the Education Agency conducting a school or institution, the Governing Board of the school or institution may engage in commercial activities for the purpose of raising funds, and may expend any money so raised for the benefit of the school or institution.

(2) Subsections 53(4) and (5) apply to any commercial activities undertaken by a Governing Board.

	(3) The Governing Board must keep proper accounts of any funds that are raised.

	(2) The Secretary is to approve and monitor the expenditure of such grants by schools and institutions.

- (b) may refuse to approve expenditure by a school or institution if the Secretary regards it as discretionary.

	(2) The school fee subsidy must be taken from the National Government Grants to the Autonomous Bougainville Government and specified for that purpose and must be paid to the schools or institutions as soon as practicable.

#### 57. SUSPENSION OF TEACHING.

(1) After consultation with an Education Agency conducting a school or institution, the Bougainville Education Board may, by written notice to the Education Agency, and the Governing Board of the school or institution, order the suspension of teaching at the school or institution if:

- (a) there is a lack of community support for the school or institution; or

- (b) there is a threat to the safety of staff or students of the school or institution due to an emergency situation; or

- the head teacher of the school or institution has failed to comply with a lawful direction given by an Education Authority.

(2) The Bougainville Education Board must, within 14 days after making an order under subsection (1), provide a written report to the Bougainville Executive Council setting out the reasons for and the period of the suspension.

(3) After consideration of the report referred to in subsection (2), the Bougainville Executive Council may:

- (a) lift the suspension; or

- (b) confirm the suspension; or

- (c) extend the period of the suspension; or

- (d) make an order under paragraph 49(1)(b); or

- (e) take such other action as the Council considers appropriate in the circumstances.

## PART 8 MISCELLANEOUS.

#### 59. APPEALS.

	(1) A person or body aggrieved by a decision of the Bougainville Education Board may appeal to the Minister within 28 days after the decision is made.

(2) If a student is expelled from a school or institution by the Governing Board of the school or institution, the student or a person on his or her behalf may appeal to the Bougainville Education Board within 28 days after the expulsion decision is made.

(3) The Minister or the Bougainville Education Board, as the case requires:

- (a) may confirm, vary or revoke the decision appealed against; and

- (b) must give the appellant written notice of his or her or its decision within 7 days after making the decision.

(4) The decision of the Minister or the Bougainville Education Board, as the case requires, is final.

(5) The regulations may prescribe matters relating to appeals.

#### 60. RESERVED TEACHING POSITIONS.

(1) An Education Agency conducting a member school or member institution may apply in writing to the Bougainville Education Board to reserve a head teacher's or teacher's position in the school or institution:

- (a) for a category of persons of a particular religious denomination or of a particular religious teaching order; or

- (b) that is defined by reference to criteria that are not commonly used in a secular education system, but are acceptable to the Bougainville Education Board.

(2) If a reserved teaching position remains vacant for twelve months, the reservation of the position lapses, unless the Bougainville Education Board decides otherwise, and the position becomes a non-reserved position.

(3) An Education Agency that applies under subsection (1) must forward to the Teaching Service Commission a copy of the application.

#### 61. OBSTRUCTIONS, ETC.

(1) A person who hinders or obstructs an Education Authority or any other person in the exercise or performance of its or his or her powers, functions, duties or responsibilities under this Act is guilty of an offence.

Penalty: A fine not exceeding K5,000.00

#### 62. EDUCATION AGENCIES AND GOVERNING BOARDS TO KEEP PROPER ACCOUNTS.

(1) All Education Agencies and Governing Boards must:

- (a) keep proper accounts and records of their transactions and of all funds; and

- (b) do all things necessary to ensure that all payments out of their monies are correctly made and properly authorised and that adequate control is maintained over their assets and the incurring of liabilities.

- (a) in the case of accounts and records kept by an Education Agency, by the Bougainville Education Board; and

- (b) in the case of accounts and records kept by a Governing Board of a school or institution, by the Education Agency conducting the school or institution, and the Bougainville Education Board.

#### 63. HOLIDAYS FOR SCHOOLS AND INSTITUTIONS.

(1) All days that are holidays in the Public Service are holidays for schools or institutions.

	(2) After giving notice to the Bougainville Education Board, an Education Agency may declare a day or part of a day to be a holiday in all or any of the schools or institutions conducted by the Agency, but such holidays must not exceed a total of three days in any one calendar year.

(3) For the purposes of subsection (2), a half day-holiday (whether in the morning or in the afternoon) is to be counted as half a day.

(4) All other holidays for schools or institutions are as prescribed.

## PART 9 TRANSITIONAL AND APPLICATION.

#### 65. RECOGNITION OF MEMBERSHIP OF EDUCATION BOARDS, ETC.

(1) This section applies to a person if the person, immediately before the commencement of this Act, was a member or alternate member appointed under the *PNG Education Act* of:

- (a) the Bougainville Education Board; or

- (b) a governing body; or

- (c) a committee of a governing body.

(2) On the commencement of this Act, the person is deemed to have been appointed under this Act as a member or alternate member of the Bougainville Education Board, the corresponding Governing Board or committee, as the case requires, for the balance of the period of his or her appointment and on the same terms and conditions.

#### 66. RECOGNITION OF EDUCATION AGENCY.

(1) This section applies to an Education Agency if, immediately before the commencement of this Act, the Education Agency was conducting a school within the meaning of the *PNG Education Act* in Bougainville.

(2) On the commencement of this Act, the Education Agency is deemed to have been recognized under this Act as an Education Agency for the purposes of the Bougainville Education System and this Act.

#### 67. RECOGNITION AS MEMBER SCHOOL, ETC.

(1) This section applies to a school if, immediately before the commencement of this Act, the school was in Bougainville and recognized as a member school within the meaning of the *PNG Education Act*.

(2) On the commencement of this Act, the school is deemed to have been recognized as a member school or member institution, as the case requires, for the purposes of the Bougainville Education System and this Act.

#### 68. AUTONOMOUS BOUGAINVILLE GOVERNMENT AS EDUCATION AGENCY.

For the purposes of this Act and any other law relating to education matters, the Autonomous Bougainville Government on the date of commencement of this Act becomes the Education Agency for all of the schools and institutions conducted by the Autonomous Bougainville Government within the Bougainville Education System.

[[END OF PAGE 49]]

[[START OF PAGE 50]]

#### 69. BOUGAINVILLE EDUCATION PLAN.

The Bougainville Education Plan that was in force immediately before the commencement of this Act continues in force on and after that commencement as if it were the Bougainville Education Plan approved under this Act.

#### 70. NON-APPLICATION OF *PNG EDUCATION ACT*.

(1) Subject to this Act, the *PNG Education Act* ceases to apply in Bougainville on the date of commencement of this Act.

(2) Despite subsection (1), any subordinate instrument made under the *PNG Education Act* that was in force immediately before the commencement of this Act relating to:

- (a) national examinations; or

- (b) curriculum development; or

- (c) teacher training; or

- (d) the registration of schools and institutions; or

- (e) any other prescribed matter;

continues to apply in Bougainville on and after that commencement until a subordinate instrument is made under this Act in substitution for it.

(3) The powers and functions relating to education matters under the *PNG Education Act* that were delegated before the commencement of this Act to the Education Authorities in Bougainville cease to apply on the commencement of this Act.
