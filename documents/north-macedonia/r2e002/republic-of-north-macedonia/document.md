## Non-discrimination  and  equal  opportunities  in  education in the context of Sustainable Development Goal 4 (SDG4)

Articles/Paragraphs  1(1),  2  and  3  of  the  Convention/Recommendation provide  for  non-discrimination  and  equal  opportunities  in  education.  This part relates to the main purpose of the Convention/Recommendation that is to underscore the state obligation to proscribe any form of discrimination in education while promoting equality of educational opportunity.

This corresponds to the overall aspiration of SDG4 which aims to 'ensure inclusive and equitable quality education and promote lifelong learning opportunities for all' by 2030. It therefore covers all the SDG4 targets, but also specifically SDG target 4.5. 4

(2)Inclusive education is a process that takes into account the diverse individual needs for development of students, by ensuring equal opportunities for exercising the fundamental human rights to development and quality education.

(3)Inclusive education from paragraph (2) of this Article shall entail changes and adjustments to the content, structures and strategies of the teaching process for students  with  disabilities,  along  with  a  common  vision  and  conviction  that  the state is under the obligation to provide education to all children.

- Inclusion  of  all  children  in  the  mainstream  primary  education  is  defined according  to  the  Concept  for  inclusive  education,  which  is  adopted  by  the Minister  on  proposal  by  the  Bureau  and  is  published  on  the  websites  of  the Ministry of education and science (hereinafter: Ministry) and the Bureau.

- The infrastructure, individualized support, curriculum and program in primary education shall be reasonably accommodated to the individual needs of a student. (6)Reasonable  accommodation  from  paragraph  (5)  of  this  Article  shall  entail changing and adjusting the conditions for education in certain cases, which does not  present  disproportional  or  unnecessary  burden  for  the  school,  and  aims  at ensuring the enjoyment or realization of all human right and freedoms of students with disabilities on an equal basis with others.

##### Accessibility of Higher Education (Article 6)

Students of all levels of higher education that have no parents, or are blind, deaf, first-  or  second-category  disabled,  mother  with  children  under  six  years  of  age and hospitalised Students shall have the right to special conveniences stipulated in the Statute of the higher education institution.

##### Exemption from Co-payment (Article 128)

- The  public  higher  education  institution  shall  not  charge  co-payment  to  the following:

- Persons without parents under the age of 26, if they are unemployed;

-Persons  with  disabilities,  in  accordance  with  a  Decree  adopted  by  the Government, upon a proposal of the National Council;

- Disabled war veterans;

- Persons  raised  in  foster  care  homes  for  abandoned  children,  if  they  are unemployed; and

- Other persons, as defined in the Government Decree adopted upon a proposal of the National Council.

The  university,  or  the  autonomous  higher  vocational  school,  determines  the procedure for selection of candidates for enrolment in a manner that guarantees equality of all candidates irrespective of race, colour, gender, language, religion, political  or  other  belief,  ethnic,  national  or  social  origin,  property,  birth,  social status, disability, sexual orientation and age.
