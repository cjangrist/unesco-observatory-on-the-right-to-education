## Article (1) Definitions

The following words and phrases stated in the decree shall have the meanings assigned below unless stated otherwise:

Ministry: Ministry of Education and Higher Education

Minister: Minister of Education and Higher Education

Directorate: Directorate of Education in educational district.

Educational Institution : All governmental and non-governmental kindergartens, schools and cultural centers which provide educational services.

Kindergartens: Every educational institution, which provides education service for children prior to primary education stage.

School: Every educational institution that includes one part or more of primary and secondary education stages where one teacher or more provide formal education services for children.

Cultural center: Every education institution that provides students with training on any type or types of knowledge or skills, without age or time limitations.

Vocational education school: Every education institution that teaches one type or more of educational materials and professional skills. It is administered by the Ministry or by another governmental institution under the supervision of the Ministry.

Governmental education institution: Every educational institution administered by the ministry or any other governmental institution under the supervision of the Ministry.

Private educational institution : Every licensed non-governmental education institution that teaches students according to the Palestinian curricula.

Foreign educational institution: Every licensed non-governmental education institution that does not teach students the Palestinian curriculum or most of its curriculum are non-Palestinian.

Student: A person who studies at an educational institution or receiving educational service from it.

## Article (2) Scope of Application

The provisions of this Decree-Law shall apply to:

- Educational institutions operating in Palestine.

- Palestinian educational institutions abroad, licensed by the Ministry, without contradictions to the  laws and regulations of the host States.

## Article (3) Education System Goals

The education system aims to achieve the following:

- Upgrading teachers' scientific and social status for their distinct role in building people and society.

- Developing student's personality, talents, cognitive and physical abilities as well as staying abreast to the informational technology.

- Preparing students to become active members in building their society,  developing their ability of objective critical analysis, adopting  a scientific approach of learning, researching and problem solving and enabling them to be scientifically and professionally well-prepared.

## Article (4) Ministry's Responsibilities

The Ministry shall have the following responsibilities:

- Providing general policies and plans to develop educational work in conformance with the national development plan.

- Providing policies that would activate the relationship between the educational institution and its local communities, establishing guardians and teachers councils and community education councils, revitalizing the activities which serve the society and consolidate the role of the education institution with the community.

- Establishing or supporting planning, development, creativity and research centers to stay abreast of scientific and educational developments.

- Using ITC to ensure education environment where modern scientific and educational methodology is used.

- Establishing and developing governmental education institutions of all types and levels.

- Providing educational opportunities for all students regardless of their  individual differences, tendencies and performance levels, including students with disabilities , juveniles, abused children, dropouts or social cases and preparing creativity-conducive environment.

- Providing  appropriate pedagogic and educational methods for all students, including the gifted, high achievers and those with disabilities.

- Providing buildings according to the specifications and standards appropriate for the use of educational institutions and respond to age categories, disabilities, students' individual differences and the objectives of the curricula and educational process.

- Providing a staff with academic and professional competences that enable them to operate and administer the governmental educational institution.

- Supervising non-governmental educational institutions.

- Holding public exams, and assessment and evaluation examinations to develop the educational system.

- Ensuring equal opportunities for the teachers working at the educational institutions to develop their scientific and pedagogical skills in coordination with the competent authorities.

## Article (5) Compulsory Education

- Education shall be compulsory up to the end of the 10th grade (primary education stage).

- Education shall be free in all governmental education institutions.

## Article (6) Education Stages

The general education system consists of the following stages:

- Kindergartens (not more than 2 years before primary education).

- Primary education (9 years).

- Secondary education (3 years).

## Article (7) Preschool Education (Kindergartens)

- The Ministry shall establish kindergartens in accordance with certain standards and training and rehabilitation centers for the educators of this stage upon a decision by the Minister.

- Kindergartens shall commit themselves to obtaining licensing from the ministry. The Minister shall issue necessary instructions to license kindergarten and organize them administratively and technically.

- The Ministry shall work to expand its preschool services to make this education stage compulsory in accordance with a standardized systemic framework approved by the ministry.

## Article (8) Pre-school Stage Goals

Pre-school stage aims to achieve the following:

- Providing an environment that contributes to developing the physical, mental, emotional and social aspects of the child and preparing them for joining primary stage.

- Providing children with basic life skills and good health habits.

- Familiarizing children with the surrounding environment and presenting them with adequate opportunities for developing their capacities.

## Article (9) Primary Education

- Primary education covers grade (1) to grade (9).

- The parents or guardians shall be obligated to enroll children under their guardianship or custodianship in primary schools.

- The Ministry shall integrate technical and vocational education into the schooling program from grade 6 to grade 9 in a manner that would ensure students acquisition of the necessary technical experiences and skills.

## Article (10) School Admission Age

- The child who is admitted to the first primary grade must be (5) years and (7) months of age or before 31st August of that school year.

- The admission age for students with disabilities shall be determined according to instructions issued by the minister in conformance with the relevant international standards and conventions, following coordination with the relevant authorities.

- The Ministry shall request from the Ministry of the Interior to provide it with the newborns data to  follow  up  the  situation  of  the  students  who  meet  entry  requirements  with  the  relevant authorities.

## Article (11) Secondary Education

- The secondary stage includes the tenth, eleventh and twelfth grades, including different streams thereof.

- Secondary education is divided into:

- Academic stream;

- Vocational and technical stream.

- The Minister shall issue the necessary instructions to determine the majors and the admission conditions to the different streams of secondary education.

## Article (12) General Examinations

- The ministry holds the following examinations:

- General Secondary Certificate Examination (Al-Injaz) at the end of the secondary stage.

- General examinations to improve educational quality.

- The results of the general secondary examination are final and can't be challenged in courts.

- Dates for general secondary examinations (Al-Injaz), employees' awards and employment tests are determined according to the instructions of the minister.

## Article (13) Education Acceleration

- The Ministry shall follow up, in cooperation with the educational institutions, gifted and talented students and determine the appropriate education programs to sponsor and teach them.

- The education process shall be accelerated and schooling years shall be advanced for the students who demonstrate creative abilities beyond their age and qualify them to skip one grade to a higher one or to a whole stage upon a decision by the Minister.

## Article (14) Inclusive Education

The Ministry shall exercise the policy of inclusive education and allied education which meet all students' needs by ensuring:

- Quality education for vulnerable students, including those with disabilities.

- Accommodated school buildings, education resources and specialized qualified teaching staffs.

- Appropriate education curricula and resilient assessment mechanisms which respond to students' individual needs and guarantee them quality education.

## Article (15) Licensing Private and Foreign Educational Institutions

- Private and foreign educational institutions shall obtain a license from the Ministry prior to their establishment.

- Private and foreign education institutions and their licensing procedures shall be determined according to a regulation issued by the Council of Ministers.

- Private and foreign educational institutions shall have independent premises. They can neither add new facilities nor establish housing units within the institution, nor transfer the institution from its location without a decision by the Minister.

## Article (16) Licensing Procedures

- The Minister shall issue a decision licensing private and foreign educational institutions. The license shall be issued in the name of the institution's owner.

- Private or foreign educational institution shall submit the establishment and license application to the education district directorate within a period of six months before the start of the schooling year.

- Private educational institutions may prepare the students, who wish so, for foreign certificates examinations that are equivalent to the general secondary education certificate on the proviso that they obtain an agreement in writing from the ministry.

## Article (23) Establishment of the Directorate

The Ministry shall establish a directorate in each educational district in accordance with its need and the approved organizational structure. This directorate shall be headed by the Education Director, assisted by administrative and technical staff.

## Article (24) School's Organizational Structure

The Ministry shall consider the school as an administrative unit with an organizational structure. Each unit shall be administered by a principal assisted by competent staff selected according to the organizational structure of the school and professional standards.

## Article (25) Teacher Recruitment

- University graduates who have educational qualification and direct connection with students and curricula shall be appointed. Holders of diploma certificate can also be appointed depending on the needs of the Ministry.

- School principal, deputy principal and educational supervisor shall be selected from among those who have education experiences and necessary qualifications in accordance with the instructions of the Minister.

- The Ministry shall determine the conditions and experiences which the teachers should meet. It shall also determine the standards which administrative staffers should meet.

## Article (30) Job Nature Allowance

- Teachers of governmental schools shall have a job nature allowance commensurate with the rank they have.

- The ministry shall prepare in coordination with the competent authorities a list of professional categories for the employees of the Ministry. It shall be issued by the Council of Ministers.

## Article (31) Certification of Non-Conviction

The educational institution shall not appoint any person who has been convicted in a competent court of a felony or a misdemeanor that constitutes a breach of honor or trust unless he \ she was acquitted.

## Article (32) Code of Conduct

The employees of the Ministry shall commit to the ethics of the profession of education issued by the Ministry and the code of conduct issued by the Council of Ministers.

## Article (33) Advisory Council

- An advisory council for General education shall be established in accordance with the provisions of this Decree Law.

- The council shall consist of (18) members from different governmental and relevant educational, social and economic institutions. They shall be appointed by the Council of Ministers upon nomination by the minister.

- The council shall be chaired by the minister.

## Article (34) Advisory Council Responsibilities

- The council shall provide the Minister with the recommendations and advice needed to upgrade the educational process.

- The Council of Ministers shall issue a regulation determining the responsibilities and mechanisms of the Advisory Council.

## Article (35) Education Curricula

The ministry shall develop the educational curricula for all education stages, based on the following:

- The national legislations and international conventions on human rights.

- Community philosophy and national, religious, moral and humane values which promote citizenship and democracy.

- Keeping abreast of the scientific, technical, economic and cultural developments.

- being responsive and flexible to the individual needs of the gifted students and high achievers as well as those with disabilities.

- Promoting healthy habits and behaviors as well as everyday life skills.

## Article (36) School Textbooks

- The Ministry shall provide duty-free textbooks for the students of the governmental education institutions at the beginning of each semester, including books for students with vision impairment (Braille System) and large print books, except for foreign languages textbooks.

- The Ministry shall set the prices of the English Language or any other foreign language textbooks taught as part of the Palestinian curricula throughout the various educational stages.

- The Ministry may distribute Palestinian curricula books for free - for the educational institutions in accordance with the standards it puts thereat.

## Article (37) Activities

The activities included in school text books shall be organized in accordance with the educational objectives which match students' educational capacities with the ministry setting forth the necessary programs and plans in its annual plan.

## Article (38) Students Performance Appraisal

- Students' performance and achievement shall be evaluated according to the modern trends of the educational appraisal system and in light of the practical teaching and learning objectives which focus on producing knowledge and genuine learning. It includes all educational levels according to the directives issued by the Minister.

- The Minister shall issue instructions specific to students' discipline in schools.

## Article (39) Use of Educational Institutions Buildings

Governmental educational institutions premises and facilities may be used after official working hours for social, cultural and entertainment purposes after the Ministry's approval.

## Article (40) Cultural Center

- With no prejudice to the mandate of the Ministry of Culture or any other entity, the cultural center shall commit itself to obtaining licensure from the Ministry to train students and provide them with knowledge and skills. The Ministry shall ratify the certificates it issues.

- The work and licensure conditions of this center shall be organized based on instructions issued by the Minister.

## Article (41) Community Organizations

Education is considered to be a joint responsibility for all CBOs, so the Ministry shall:

- Establish students' guardians' councils whose roles shall be determined according to instructions issued by the Minister.

- Cooperate with all national and international organizations working in Palestine to promote the teaching and learning process within an effective participatory framework.

## Article (42) Programs and Plans

- The Ministry shall set forth necessary programs, according to the needs of dropouts or the students with absences due to compelling conditions determined by the Ministry, to be re-enrolled in the school system in coordination with the relevant authorities.

- The school sets the programs and plans aimed at improving students' achievement and performance in cooperation with relevant experts and educators on the level of the community and civil society organizations and in accordance with instructions issued by the minister.

## Article (43) Services and Productive Activities

The educational  institution may  provide  paid  productive  services  and  activities  for  individuals, institutions and community for the purpose of developing its facilities and rewarding its students and employees. Spending shall be conducted in accordance with instructions issued by the Minister.

## Article (44) Government's Obligations

The government shall fund the programs and plans of the general education based on the proposals of the ministry to enforce the obligations set forth in this Decree Law.

## Article (45) Fundraising

Governmental educational institutions may raise funds at the start of the school year with a view to develop and improve the quality of education in line with a regulation issued by the Council of Ministers.

## Article (46) Grants and Aid

- The Ministry shall accept unconditional financial and in-kind grants, aid and donations provided by states and international and local organizations or persons to implement projects and programs that help achieve the educational objectives in accordance with the provisions of this Decree Law and the financial regulations in effect.

- The Ministry can't receive any grants or assistances from unreliable sources or for the purpose of causing commotion among the students or the employees or the community. It can't either cooperate with entities in conflict with the national interest and goals of the Palestinian society which seeks to attain its freedom from the occupation.

## Article (47) Refraining from Promoting or Encouraging Any Activity with an Enemy Entity

The educational institution shall not promote or encourage any activity with any entity the objectives of which are directed against the interest of the Palestinian society in any mean.

## Article (51) Education in Emergencies

The Ministry shall provide education in emergencies and maintain the minimum standards of the quality of the education services under such conditions. It shall also cooperate with local, regional and international organizations which the State doesn't ban or their activities don't conflict with the national interest of the people of Palestine.

## Article (52) Instructions Validity Period

The instructions issued by the Ministry pursuant to the previous applicable laws shall remain in force unless they are revoked, amended or substituted.

## Article (53) Ratification of Legal Status

The licensure decision enforced before issuance of this Decree Law shall continue to be valid as if it were issued in accordance with this Decree Law. All education institutions should rectify their status within period of (2) years as maximum.

## Article (54) Evaluating the Implications of the Legislations

The Ministry shall assess the implications of the regulations, instructions, policies and decisions by conducting specialized educational studies and researches. It shall also set forth the instructions related to encouraging and motivating achievement and excellence as well as accountability for any error in the teaching learning process.

## Article (55) Issuance of Regulations and Instruction

- The Council of Ministers issues the necessary regulations to enforce the provisions of this Decree Law within a period of (6) months from the date it takes effect.
