This is an opportunity for the institutions to appoint social workers, educational mediators or teacher's assistants, as well as to engage staff responsible for the implementation of the measures to support access to education and for preventing the risk of dropping out of the pre-school and school educational system. Increasing the coverage and improving the quality of pre-school education is a priority policy of the Ministry of Education and Science in view of the critical importance of pre-school education for personal and social wellbeing. In that regard, the National Program 'Development of the Pre-School Education System' was implemented in the 2017/2018 and 2018/2019 school years in kindergartens and schools with pre-school groups. The main activity under the program concerns additional Bulgarian language classes for children whose mother tongue is other than Bulgarian. In addition to creating conditions for optimal physical and personal development of the children, the program is also focused on encouraging the active involvement of parents in the pre-school education process. In support of this policy, the Ministry of Education and Science has been implementing the project 'Active inclusion in the pre-school education system' under Operational Programme 'Science and Education for Smart Growth' 2014-2020 (OP SESG), which partially builds on the activity under the Mechanism for prevention of early school drop-out. One of the project activities concerns Bulgarian language trainings for children who need such training. For the first time, such trainings are provided to 3- and 4-year olds. The purpose of the activity is to ensure that the Bulgarian language is mastered as an effective tool for social inclusion and as a prevention of learning problems and dropout. A major project objective is to enhance the access of children from vulnerable groups living in poverty to pre-school education. In that regard, the project envisages payment of kindergarten fees as a means to overcome the economic barriers to inclusion and eliminate one of the most important reasons for non-participation in pre-school education and as a motivation for parents for active family kindergarten cooperation. The task of the State is to ensure equal access for all children, not only for a specific group of children. Therefore, the State Budget Act provides that the Council of Ministers shall take the necessary steps, including by exercising their right to legislative initiative, to introduce compulsory pre-school education for children aged 4 and to support parents in paying the fees under Article 298(2) of the Pre-School and School Education Act. A programme for building, extension, raising superstructures and reconstruction of crèches, kindergartens and schools for the period 2020 - 2022 is adopted to support the implementation of the measure. Art. 36 (2) of the Constitution of R Bulgaria stipulates that every citizen, for whom Bulgarian is not their mother tongue, is entitled to use and study simultaneously their mother tongue alongside with Bulgarian. The Pre-School and School Education Act further provides for the conditions for this in Art. 13 (6). The government encourages the study of cultures, languages and religions present in Bulgaria. The existing legal framework for the preschool and school education system creates conditions for equal access to education to all children and students, regardless of their ethnic origin. Normative requirements are established in support of activities related to the education process of children and students for whom the Bulgarian language is not mother tongue. It should be particularly emphasised that the study of mother tongue is not conditional on any recognition of a particular minority group, but rather a matter of individual will of the persons concerned. The study of mother tongue is possible in all Bulgarian schools in the form of obligatory eligible subject and/or freely eligible subject. The only requirement is that parents file an application for the particular language with the school administration. The textbooks are free of charge. For the time being, curricula for study of Turkish, Hebrew and Armenian as mother tongues for 1st - 8th grades have been approved, and curricula for study of Roma native language have been designed for 2nd - 8th grades. After completing the project activities in which educational mediators are included, the necessary analysis shall be performed and the next measures shall be taken on the basis of the lessons learnt and the current need for sustainability of their activities.
