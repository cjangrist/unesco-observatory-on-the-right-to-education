### A. Laws and policies (preamble and paras 4 and 7 of the 1974 Recommendation )

#### A1a. Please  describe any  specific national or sub-national laws, legislation or legal framework(s) in your country that govern the promotion of global citizenship education (GCED) and/or education for sustainable development (ESD). Explain whether GCED and/or ESD themes 1  are mentioned explicitly or are inferred and what types or levels of education are covered.

The following policy documents are main documents in terms of supporting global citizenship education (GCED) and education for sustainable development (ESD).

- Presidency Annual Programs: Education policies are determined on the axis of sustainable development goals.

- 11th Development Plan: Education policies under the title of 'Education' constitute a source as a framework for long-term policy objectives and other policy documents.

- Ministry of National Education 2023 Education Vision: It is the policy document that plans the transformation of the Turkish education system.

- MoNE 2019-2023 Strategic Plan: Policy objectives are determined by giving priority to sustainable development.

- In the Basic Law of National Education No.1739, there are some objectives that aims to raise individuals who are respectful to human rights and responsible for the society, and to ensure that individuals have a profession that will make them happy and contribute to the happiness of the society.

- Vocational Education Law No. 3308

- Higher Education Law No. 2547

- MoNE Regulation on Secondary Education Institutions

- MoNE Regulation on Lifelong Learning Institutions

- National Action Plan to Combat Violence Against Women

- National Program for Combating Child Labor

While determining educational policies and objectives that will serve sustainable development education, the above main policy documents are taken as a basis and studies based on these main policies and objectives are carried out in every field of education.

The official governmental entity to follow the issues about sustainable development goals and actions are the Presidency of Strategy and Budget. The website of the organization is given in A1b.

### B. Curriculum (paras 4 and 10-19 of the 1974 Recommendation )

#### B1a. Please describe in which ways GCED and ESD are mainstreamed in school curricula. Indicate  whether  topics  are  taught  as  standalone  subjects  or  are  integrated  into  the curricula of other subjects. Explain whether GCED and/or ESD themes 3  are mentioned explicitly or are inferred and what types or levels of education are covered.

- There is no standalone subjects under the names of Global Citizenship Education (GCED) and / or Sustainable Development Education (ESD) in our country. However, many of the themes listed under the aforementioned headings are included in our national curricula applied at pre-primary, primary, secondary and upper secondary education levels. These themes are covered in curricula, explicitly and / or implicitly, under a single discipline and / or with interdisciplinary approaches.

- Curricula for primary and lower-secondary have teaching and learning of key competences part  of  their  lifelong  learning  strategies.  All  Curricula,  like  languages,  mathematics, science,  identifies  8  key  competences  that  are  fundamental  for  each  individual  in  a knowledge-based society in Turkey schools systems. Of these 8 key competences related to GCED and ESD are as follows.

-Social and civic competences: ability to participate effectively and constructively in one's social and working life and engage in active and democratic participation, especially in increasingly diverse societies.

-Sense of initiative and entrepreneurship: ability to turn ideas into action through creativity, innovation and risk taking as well as ability to plan and manage projects.

-Cultural awareness and expression: ability to appreciate the creative importance of ideas, experiences and  emotions in a range of media such as music, literature  and visual and performing arts.

- On the other hand, some courses' curricula have themes or units related to GCED and ESD (like  social  science,  life  science)  while  some  of  the  courses  made  of  separate  subjects related to GCED and ESD directly (like human rights and citizenship, law and justice)

- There is a lot of non-formal education program in Turkey about the SDGs. Each individual can take all of these courses in the public education centers. All of these educations and trainings are free of charge. A certificate is provided at the end of the education. Men and women can take these education, there is no limitation.

#### B1b. Please provide examples of curricula in which GCED and/or ESD are mainstreamed and, where possible, web links to relevant documents.

In all our curricula, under the title of Competencies, there are "social and civic competencies" which are aimed to be acquired by our students. In this context, students are equipped to fully participate in civil life based on knowledge of social and political concepts and structures, and a commitment to democratic and active participation.

In addition, for instance, among the aims of the Social Studies Curriculum (Primary School and Secondary School 4th, 5th, 6th and 7th Grades) is that students " to know the importance and ways of being a virtuous person by adopting national, moral values and universal values".
