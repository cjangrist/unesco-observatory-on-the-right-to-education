# LAW OF TURKMENISTAN ON THE GUARANTEES OF THE RIGHTS OF THE CHILD

The present Law is aimed at implementing main principles of the policy of President of Turkmenistan Saparmurat Turkmenbashi in the sphere of human rights and identijles legal status of the child as independent subject, regulates guarantees of the child rights and legal interests as well as provides for physical and spiritual development of the child, forming his/her citizenship awareness on the basis of the national and universal values common to all mankind.

## Chapter I. General Provisions

### Article 1. Child

A  child  means  every  human  being  below  the  age  of  18  years  unless  the  Law  of Turkmenistan establishes other.

### Article 2. Legislature of Turkmenistan on the rights of the child

Legislature of Turkmenistan on the rights of the child is based on the Constitution of Turkmenistan,  the  present Law,  other  legal  norms  and  acts  of  Turkmenistan, international agreements of Turkmenistan and relevant norms of international law.

### Article 3. Parity of rights

- All children living on the territory of Turkmenistan have equal rights, irrespective of the child's or his or her parent's or legal guardian's nationality, race, sex, language, religion, social origin, property or other status, education and place of living, birth, disability or other status.

- The child has the right to enjoy all entitled rights and freedoms. Implementation of the rights and freedoms shall not be harmful for the life, health, education and entire development of the child.

- Laws and other legal norms and acts of Turkmenistan, as well as universally recognised principles and norms of international law shall ensure equal rights for the child.

### Article 4. State policy for the interests of the child

- State policy of Turkmenistan for the interests of the child provides for:

-legal support of rights and legal interests of the child, prohibition of discrimination and restoration of rights in case of their violation;

-development and implementation of state target programmes aimed at ensuring the rights and legal interests of the child and motherhood and childhood support;

-development of state social norms for living standards of the child;

- While implementing actions for the state policy for the interests of the children there shall be a possibility to form state order for the production of the items and services for children.

### Article 5. Financial support of the State policy for the interests of the child

Financial support of the State policy for the interests of the child regarding protection of the child's rights and legal interests and motherhood and childhood support shall be implemented at the expense of the State Budget of Turkmenistan and non-budget sources attracted for the above mentioned purposes in accordance with the legislature of Turkmenistan.

## Chapter II. Main rights and freedoms of the child

### Article 6. Right to life and development

- Every child has the inherent right to life and adequate conditions for his or her physical, mental and spiritual development.

- The State and relevant entities shall take legal, economic, social and other measures to ensure adequate conditions for the life and development of the child, healthy and safe environment for his or her living.

### Article 7. Right to identity and its preservation

- From the moment of the birth the child has the right to preserve his or her identity, including citizenship, nationality, name, family name and family relations.

- The fact of the child's birth shall be registered in accordance with the procedure by the Civil Status Record and Registration body.

### Article 8. Right to essential living standard

- No child shall be left without minimum means for survival as well as without guardianship.

- The right of the child to essential living standard shall be ensured by the parents (legal representatives), authorities and state administration bodies.

### Article 9. Right to live in the family

- Every child has the right to live in the family together with his or her parents (legal representatives) and shall not be separated from them against his or her will, except cases stipulated by the legislature of Turkmenistan.

- A child living separately from his or her parents (legal representatives) has the right to maintain personal relations with them (him or her), get information about

- The State and its relevant bodies shall make for the reunion of the family.

### Article 10. Right to private life

The right of the child to private life shall not be violated by forceful or illegal interference offending his or her honour, dignity, affections and good name of the child.

### Article 11. Right to freedom of expression, worship and religion

- Every child has the right to freedom of worship, religion and expression of his or her own views and ideas freely.

- Views and wishes of the child regarding decision of all matters affecting the child shall be given due weight in accordance with the age and maturity of the child.

### Article 12. Right to healthcare

Every child has the right to get essential medical assistance, healthcare and health strengthening.

### Article 13. Right to education

Every child has the right to education that shall promote his or her cultural and intellectual development, abilities, form his or her convictions, moral and social commitments.

### Article 14. Right to dwelling area

- No child shall be left without dwelling.

- The child -a member of the family of the dwelling tenant -has the right to live in and use dwelling area equally to other members of the family. The child -a member of the family of the dwelling owner- has the right to live in, use and inherit dwelling area equally to other members of the family. In case of the parents' deaths or other circumstances the dwelling area shall be

assigned to the child in accordance with the legal procedure.

### Article 15. Right to rest and leisure

Every child has the right to rest and leisure appropriate to his or her age and health.

### Article 16. Right to know cultural values

- Every child has the right to know history, traditions, and spiritual values of his or her nation and world culture, to freedom of artistic, scientific and technical creativity, participation in cultural life of the society, to develop his or her abilities and interests.

- The child living in Turkmenistan has the right to his or her native language, to follow cultural and folk traditions and customs.

### Article 17. Right to honour and dignity protection

The child has the right to honour and dignity protection.

### Article 18. Right to freedom of association into children and youth organisations

Children have the right to unite into children and youth association in accordance with
the legislature of Turkmenistan.

### Article 19. Right to labour

- The child has the right to labour appropriate to his age, health, education and professional training.

- The right to labour activities shall arise for the child being over 16 years old. Labour contract with the child shall be concluded if the child is over 16 years old. The child shall be able to start working at the age of 15 years with the consent of one of the parents (a legal representative).

- Conditions and procedure of the child employment, as well as advantages are established by the legislature of Turkmenistan.

### Article 20. Right to inheritance

The child has the right to inheritance in cases and according to the procedure stipulated by the legislature of Turkmenistan.

### Article 21. Right to state allowance

The child has the right to state allowance in cases and according to the procedure stipulated by the legislature of Turkmenistan.

### Article 22. Rights of the child who is not a citizen of Turkmenistan

The child not being the citizen of Turkmenistan but being legally on the territory of Turkmenistan shall have the rights and responsibilities equally with the children who are citizens of Turkmenistan unless other is stipulated by the legislature of Turkmenistan and international agreements of Turkmenistan.

## Chapter III. Guarantees and protection of the child's rights and legal interests

### Article 23. State guarantees of the child's rights

- The State shall protect the rights and legal interests of the child provided for the Constitution  of  Turkmenistan,  the  present  Law,  other  legal  norms  and  acts  of Turkmenistan,  international  agreements  of  Turkmenistan  and  relevant  norms  of international law.

- The rights and legal interests of the child shall be protected in the family by his or her  parents  (legal  representatives),  in  pre-school,  school  and  other  educational institution or specialised health rehabilitation institution by administration, as well as by authorities and state local bodies and relevant public organisations.

### Article 24. Protection of the child's right to appropriate upbringing

- Upbringing of the child in the family is the priority from ensuring the rights and legal  interests  of  the  child  in  the  best  way  and  maybe  stopped  only  on  the  basis provided for the legislature of Turkmenistan.

- Parents  (legal  representatives)  shall  be  responsible  for  taking  care  of  the  child, creating conditions for his or her growth, development and improvement, they have to upbring the child in the spirit of humanism, eternal spiritual values expressed in the holy book Rukhnama, to treat him or her as a personality in accordance to his or her individuality, age and sex.

Parents (legal representatives) shall promote  the  child's independent  activities, consistent with his or her age and capability, aimed at implementation and protection of his or her rights and legal interests.

- Humiliation  of  the  child's  dignity,  intimidation,  corporal  punishment  or  other physical abuse harmful for the child's mental and physical health is inadmissible. Both parents near equal responsibility for the life, health and appropriate upbringing of the child.

- The State shall ensure family form of upbringing to the child deprived of his or her parents' care: adoption, guardianship or placement in suitable institutions for care of children.

- The State shall ensure protection of the child from all forms of unworthy treatment by his or her parents (legal representatives) or other persons responsible for his or her upbringing and care and provide appropriate supervision over adequate conditions of guardianship.

### Article 25. Protection of the child's right to education

- The  State  shall  create  necessary  conditions  for  the  education  of  the  child  in accordance with the Constitution of Turkmenistan and the Law of Turkmenistan on Education.

- Education of the child shall provide development of his or her mental and physical abilities, personality, creative abilities and talent, aesthetic and cultural development. For these purposes the State shall encourage publication of educational, methodological literature, books, magazines and newspapers for children production of films, video films and TV programmes aimed at addressing these issues and ensure their accessibility. The child should be ready for the independent life, educated in the spirit of respect to parents, fellow-countrymen, other people, state language, national culture and customs, languages and cultures of other countries, care for nature as well as to learn how to follow rules of behaviour in the family and society.

- Institutions of general education shall ensure professional training of the children starting from the 7th form of the secondary school.

### Article 26. Protection of the child right's to healthcare

- The State shall ensure children a possibility to get free medical care in the range stipulated by the legislature of Turkmenistan.

- The State shall support financially establishment of wide net of children's facilities, clubs and other objects stimulating strengthening of the children's health.

### Article 27 Protection of the child from exploitation at work

- The  State  shall  protect  the  child  from  all  forms  of  exploitation  at  work  by  legal, economic, social, medical and educational means.

- Use of the children shall not be allowed in performing works that are likely to be hazardous  or  harmful  to  the  child's  health  or  physical,  mental,  spiritual,  moral  or social  development.  Children  are  prohibited  to  perfonn  works  related  to  production and sale of tobacco and alcohol products.

- Recruiting schoolchildren during the academic year to agriculture and other works not related to learning process shall not be allowed.

### Article 28. Protection of the child from illicit use of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances

- The State shall take all appropriate measures to advocate healthy way of living and protect the child from illicit use of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances and shall oppose illicit narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances circulation.

- The State shall ensure the drug-abused with medical assistance.

- Engagement of children into production, circulation, distribution and use of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances shall be considered as criminal in accordance with the legislature of Turkmenistan.

### Article 29. Protection of the child from obscenities

- Production and distribution of pornographic publications, films and other materials are prohibited in Turkmenistan.

- The State shall protect children from any form of sexual abuse.

### Article 30. Protection of the child from information and materials harmful for his or her spiritual and moral development

- The State shall promote the child to have access to infonnation and materials from various sources.

- Demonstration, release, sale, presenting and copying toys, films, audio and video recordings, literature, magazines, newspapers and other printing materials that directly encourage and propagandise war, cruel treatment, violence, and all forms of discrimination including racial, religious, sex and age discrimination and are harmful for spiritual and moral development of the child, are prohibited.

- Expertise of all means and materials that are likely to be harmful to the spiritual

### Article 31. Protection of the disabled child rights

- Disabled children shall have the right to enjoy a full and decent life and active participation in the community.

- The State promotes social support and adaptation of the disabled children through the educational, medical and rehabilitation institutions that the disabled children have access to education, professional training and upbringing adequate to their health. Employment of the children having professional limitations due to their health shall be perfonned by the state employment services.

- The parents (legal representatives) upbringing the disabled child shall have advantages envisaged by the legislature of Turkmenistan.

### Article 32. Protection of the child's rights in case of his or her infringement of the law

- The arrest, detention or imprisonment of the child is pennissible only in cases and according to the procedure stipulated by the legislature of Turkmenistan, with immediate notification of his or her parents on this act.

- Procedure and conditions of serving sentence in the form of his or her liberty deprivation shall be in accordance with the legislature of Turkmenistan.

### Article 33. Protection of the child's rights in special institutions

- Sending the child to special institution shall be performed in accordance with the court decision.

- Rehabilitation of the children aimed at the child's return to normal way of life is a

- compulsory condition of their custody in a special institution. The child who is kept in a special institution shall have the right to human treatment, healthcare, general and professional education, to contacts with his or

- her family, relatives and other people through visits and correspondence.

### Article 34. Protection of the child from abduction, sale and traffic

The State shall take all appropriate measures to prevent the abduction of, the sale of or traffic in children for any purpose or in any form.

## Chapter IV. Duties and limits of the child's rights

### Article 35. Duties of the child

- The child has the following duties:

-to respect and follow the Constitution of Turkmenistan, legal norms and acts of Turkmenistan;

-to  respect  and  take  care  of  national  traditions,  cultural  and  historical  values and nature;

-to be educated in the spirit of fulfilment duties and be personally responsible for his or her behaviour;

-to get general secondary education in accordance with the Law of Turkmenistan on Education;

-to get knowledge and prepare himself or herself to working activities;

-to respect parents (legal representatives) and other members of the family, take care of them when they get old, sick or other circumstances related to health, to help them and other members of the family when necessary;

-to follow behaviour norms and rules in the family and society;

-to take care of own health;

-to fulfil other duties stipulated by the legislature of Turkmenistan.

- While exercising his or her rights the child shall not violate the rights and legal interests of other people.

- The child who has violated the law shall be personally responsible for his or her actions unless the law stipulates the other.

### Article 36. Limits of the child's rights

The rights of the child shall be limited only on the basis envisaged by the legislature of Turkmenistan.

## Chapter V. Final provisions

### Article 37. International co-operation

Turkmenistan takes part in the international co-operation in the sphere of the child's rights and legal interests protection, motherhood and childhood protection and ensures implementation of its international commitments in these issues.

### Article 38. International Agreements of Turkmenistan

In case the international agreements of Turkmenistan contain other provisions than the present Law, the international norms shall be prevailing.

### Article 39. Responsibility for violation of the child's rights and legal interests
Responsibility for violation of the child's rights and legal interests is regulated by the legislature of Turkmenistan.
