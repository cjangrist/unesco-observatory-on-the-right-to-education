# LAW OF TURKMENISTAN On state guarantees of the rights of the child

766 / / 12.05.2014 /   Legislation (\?rub=12)

This Law denes the legal, social, economic and organizational basis of state policy in the eld of protection of the rights, freedoms and legitimate interests of the child, determines the legal status of the child as an independent entity and is aimed at ensuring its full development, the formation of high moral qualities, education in the spirit patriotism, citizenship based on historical and national traditions, universal spiritual, moral, cultural and social values   and accepted in societies principles, rules and norms of morality, respect for public and state interests.

### Article 1. Basic concepts used in this Law

The following basic concepts are used in this Law :

1) child - a person under the age of eighteen years (of majority);

2) childhood - the period of a child's life until he reaches adulthood;

3) protection of the rights of the child - a set of measures implemented by society and the state aimed at protecting the rights, freedoms and legitimate interests of the child, ensuring the upbringing and development of the child in accordance with his developing abilities;

4) an orphan - a child left without the care of both parents or a single parent in connection with their death or the court declaring them dead;

### Article 2. Legislation of Turkmenistan on state guarantees of the rights of the child

- The legislation of Turkmenistan on state guarantees of the rights of the child is based on the Constitution of Turkmenistan and consists of this Law and other regulatory legal acts of Turkmenistan.

- If other rules are established by an international treaty of Turkmenistan than those contained in this Law, then the rules of the international treaty shall apply.

### Article 3. Scope of this Law

- The eect of this Law shall apply to relations arising in the protection of the rights, freedoms and legitimate interests of the child.

- This Law also applies to a child of a foreign citizen, a stateless person, a refugee, a refugee child residing in the territory of Turkmenistan.

### Article 4. Equality of the rights of the child

- Turkmenistan guarantees the equality of rights and freedoms of each child living in the territory of Turkmenistan, regardless of nationality, race, gender, origin, social and property status, place of residence of himself and his parents, language, education, religion, circumstances of his birth health conditions or other circumstances.

- Every child has the right to enjoy all the rights and freedoms granted to him. In the exercise of the rights, freedoms and legitimate interests of the child, the best interests of the child must be ensured.

- The equal rights of the child are ensured by laws and other regulatory legal acts of Turkmenistan, generally recognized principles and norms of international law.

- Violation of the equality of the child on the basis of sex, race, nationality, origin, social and property status, place of residence of himself and his parents, language, education, religion, circumstances of his birth, health status or other dierences shall entail liability provided for legislation of Turkmenistan.

### Article 5. Prohibition of restriction of the rights of the child

- The rights of the child may not be restricted, with the exception of cases established by the legislation of Turkmenistan.

- Any actions and (or) inaction restricting the rights and legitimate interests of the child are invalid.

### Article 6. State policy in the interests of the child

- The priority of state policy in the interests of the child is to ensure his best interests.

- State policy in the interests of the child is aimed at:

- legislative support and protection of the rights, freedoms and legitimate interests of the child;

## CHAPTER 2. RIGHTS, FREEDOMS AND OBLIGATIONS OF THE CHILD

### Article 8. State guarantees of the rights of the child

- Each child owns and is guaranteed by the state the rights and freedoms in accordance with the Constitution of Turkmenistan, this Law and other regulatory legal acts of Turkmenistan.

- Children born in wedlock and (or) out of wedlock enjoy state support, they are provided with equal and comprehensive protection of their rights, freedoms and legitimate interests.

- The state shall take the necessary measures to ensure the protection of the child from any form of discrimination.

Any discrimination based on disability or other circumstances is prohibited. The state guarantees children with disabilities equal with other children eective legal protection against discrimination on any grounds.

### Article 9. The right of the child to life and development

- Every child has the inalienable right to life from the moment he is determined to be live born and viable according to the criteria established by the health authorities, worthy conditions of physical, intellectual and spiritual development. No child can be deprived of the right to life.

- The state and its relevant bodies shall take organizational, legal, socio-economic and other measures to ensure decent living and development conditions for the child, and a healthy and safe environment for his or her living.

- The state creates conditions for ensuring the birth and development of a healthy child.

- The right of every child to free life is protected by the state on the basis of law.

### Article 10. The right of a child to a name and citizenship

- Every child from the moment of birth has the right to a name and to acquire citizenship.

- The fact and place of birth of the child are registered by state bodies of civil registration, as well as by bodies authorized to carry out state registration of acts of civil status in the manner established by the legislation of Turkmenistan.

- The right of the child to acquire and change citizenship is regulated by the legislation of Turkmenistan on citizenship.

### Article 11. The right of the child to preserve his individuality

- Every child from the moment of birth has the right to preserve his individuality, including citizenship, nationality, rst name, middle name, last name, as well as family and family ties.

- If a child illegally loses part or all of the elements of his personality, the state shall provide him with the necessary assistance and protection for the speedy restoration of his personality.

- Each child has the right to change the name, patronymic and last name in the manner prescribed by the legislation of Turkmenistan.

### Article 12. The right of the child to the necessary standard of living

- Each child has the right to the standard of living necessary for his physical, intellectual, moral, cultural, spiritual and social development.

- The parents of the child, bodies of state power and administration, and local self-government are responsible for ensuring the living conditions necessary for the full development of the child, as provided for by the legislation of Turkmenistan.

- No child may be left without a minimum means of subsistence, as well as guardianship (guardianship).

### Article 13. The right of a child to live and be raised in a family

- Every child has the right to live and be raised in a family, to know his parents, the right to care for them, to live together with them, if this does not contradict his best interests, to be raised by his parents, respect for his human dignity.

- The family is the natural environment for the physical, intellectual, moral, cultural, spiritual and social development of the child, his material support and is responsible for creating the proper conditions for this.

- A child may not be separated from his parents against his will and desire, with the exception of cases provided for by the legislation of Turkmenistan.

- Parents have equal rights and obligations in relation to their child. The main concern and direct responsibility of parents is to ensure the best interests of their child.

- Parents have the right and obligation to raise their child, take care of his health, harmonious development, education, preparation for socially useful work, instill in him respect for the laws, historical and national traditions of the Turkmen people.

- A child who does not live together with his parents has the right to maintain personal relations with them (one of them),

### Article 14. The right of the child to protect his honor and dignity

Every child has the right to protection of his honor and dignity.

### Article 15. The right of the child to privacy

- Every child has the right to privacy.

- Every child has the right to freedom of conscience, religion, free expression of his own opinion and views.

- The views and wishes of the child in resolving any issues aecting his interests are taken into account taking into account his age and maturity.

### Article 17. The right of the child to protection of health

- Every child has the right to protection and promotion of health.

- Each child has the right to use the modern services of the healthcare system of Turkmenistan, the means of treating diseases and restoring health. No child may be denied access to such services.

### Article 18. The right of the child to education

- Every child has the right to education, which is one of the basic and inalienable constitutional rights of citizens of Turkmenistan.

- Every child has the right to receive general secondary education, which is compulsory, in state educational institutions for free.

- The state encourages the development of talent and the education of the child.

### Article 19. The right of the child to proper upbringing

- Each child has the right to appropriate upbringing aimed at developing and improving the personality on the basis of historical and national traditions, universal spiritual, moral, cultural and social values, principles, rules and standards of behavior adopted in society.

### Article 20. The child's right to communicate with parents (one of them) and close relatives, including those living outside Turkmenistan, and to leave Turkmenistan

Parents of the child, taking into account his age and legal capacity, assist him in the implementation of independent actions aimed at the realization and protection of his rights, freedoms and legitimate interests.

- The state guarantees the compliance of the conditions for the upbringing, education and maintenance of the child with the requirements of the state sanitary and epidemiological rules and standards.

### Article 38. Protection of the child from exploitation at work

- The state protects the child from all forms of exploitation at work by means of a legal, economic, social, medical and educational nature.

- It is forbidden to use the labor of a child under the age of eighteen years to work with special working conditions, as well as to work, the performance of which may harm his health and moral development (gambling, production, transportation and trade in alcoholic beverages, tobacco products, drugs, toxic and other means). It is also forbidden to carry and move, manually lift and move by a child heavy loads that exceed the limit norms established by the legislation of Turkmenistan.

- It is forbidden to involve a child under the age of eighteen to work at night, work overtime, work on weekends, nonworking holidays and memorial days, and also send him on business trips.

- It is not allowed to involve a child during the school year in agricultural and other work related to weaning him from school.

### Article 39. Protection of a child from illegal use of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances, consumption of tobacco products

- The state shall take the necessary measures to promote a healthy lifestyle and protect the child from illegal use of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances, consumption of tobacco products, and counteracts their illegal tracking.

- The involvement of the child in the production, tracking, distribution and use of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances, the consumption of tobacco products entails liability established by the legislation of Turkmenistan.

- The state shall prevent diseases associated with the use of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances, the consumption of alcohol, tobacco products and exposure to tobacco smoke, ensure that medical institutions provide medical assistance to a child who needs treatment for tobacco dependence and the consequences of the consumption of tobacco products, alcohol, and guarantees a child suering from drug addiction and substance abuse, the provision of narcological assistance.

- Advertising and demonstration of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances, tobacco products and their consumption in newly created audiovisual works, including television, video and cartoons, in theatrical and entertainment performances, in television, video and lm programs, as well as public execution, communication, in which the demonstration of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances, tobacco products and their consumption.

### Article 40. Protection of a child from obscenity

- In Turkmenistan, the manufacture, distribution and advertising of pornographic print media, lms, videos and cartoons or other objects and means of a pornographic nature, as well as involving the child in the manufacture, distribution and advertising of such products, is prohibited.

- The state shall protect children from any assault and sexual violence.

### Section 42. Protection of the rights of a child with a disability
1. A child with a disability in Turkmenistan has the full range of socio-economic, personal rights and freedoms enshrined in the Constitution and laws of Turkmenistan. A child with a disability has the right to a worthy and full participation in the life of society and the state. 2. Discrimination of a child with a disability is prohibited. A child with a disability has the right to respect for his physical and mental integrity on an equal basis with other children. Persons admitting discrimination are liable in accordance with the legislation of Turkmenistan. 3. The state shall take all necessary measures to ensure the full enjoyment by a child with a disability, taking into account his individual abilities and interests of all human rights and fundamental freedoms on an equal basis with other children, respect for the developing abilities of a child with disabilities and his right to preserve his personality. 4. In all activities regarding a child with a disability, priority is given to the best interests of the child. 5. The state shall ensure the right of a child with a disability to freely express his own views on all matters relating to him, relevant to his age and degree of maturity, on an equal basis with other children, to receive assistance in accordance with his disability and age to exercise this right. 6. In order to socially support and adapt a child with a disability, the state creates an enabling environment that provides him with access to educational, medical and cultural institutions, public transport, etc., promotes his full and eective participation in society on an equal basis with other children. 7. The state guarantees a child with a disability social protection in the form of material support and social services through the payment of state benets and social benets in the manner prescribed by the legislation of Turkmenistan. 8. Parents raising a child with a disability are provided with benets provided for by the legislation of Turkmenistan.

### Article 43. Maintenance and upbringing of orphans and children left without parental care

- The state shall ensure the maintenance and upbringing of orphans and children left without parental care, keeping a centralized record of such children in the manner established by the legislation of Turkmenistan.

- An orphaned child or a child left without parental care, in his best interests, shall be transferred to close relatives in the family for adoption.

- In case the child cannot be provided with a family form of education, he is transferred under guardianship (trusteeship), guardian (curator) or sent to the appropriate child care institution, social service institution for full state support. Pupils of such institutions are provided with the necessary conditions for comprehensive and harmonious development, preparation for independent life and work.

- An orphaned child and a child left without parental care shall have the rights provided for by this Law and other regulatory legal acts of Turkmenistan.

- The procedure for transferring such children for adoption, under guardianship (guardianship), monitoring the conditions of their residence and education in the families of adoptive parents, as well as those under guardianship (guardianship), is determined by the legislation of Turkmenistan.

- Intermediary, commercial activities on the adoption of children, their transfer under guardianship (trusteeship) or on raising families of citizens of Turkmenistan, foreign citizens or stateless persons are prohibited.

- It is not allowed to receive unjustied nancial benets as a result of adoption or guardianship of a orphan or child left without parental care.

### Article 44. Protection of the rights of the child in case of bringing him to justice

- Detention, arrest, other restriction or imprisonment of a child is allowed only in cases and in the manner prescribed by the legislation of Turkmenistan, with immediate notication of this to his parents.

- The procedure and conditions for children to serve sentences of imprisonment are established by the legislation of Turkmenistan.

### Article 45. Protection of the child from abduction and tracking.
The

state shall take all necessary measures to prevent the abduction and sale of the child for any purpose and in any form.

### Article 46. Rights, duties and responsibilities of parents for the upbringing and development of the child

- Parenting in the family is the basis for the harmonious development of the personality of the child.

- Each parent has the same responsibility for the upbringing, education and development of the child, and the state provides them with support in this.

- Parents of the child have the right and obligation to raise the child, take care of his health, physical, spiritual and moral development, education, create the right conditions for the disclosure of his natural abilities, respect the dignity of the child, prepare him for independent life and work, and the state provides appropriate help parents in raising a child.

- The state provides parents with assistance in fullling their responsibilities for raising a child, protects the rights of the

- Parents, taking into account the legal capacity and age of the child, provide him with assistance in the implementation of independent actions aimed at the realization and protection of his rights and legitimate interests.

- Parents of the child in accordance with the legislation of Turkmenistan are responsible for violation and (or) restriction of the rights, freedoms and legitimate interests of the child related to the protection of health, physical and spiritual development, education, as well as for non-fulllment or evasion of parental duties.

- In case of refusal to provide the child with the necessary medical care, if this threatens his health, the parents of the child, as well as medical workers, are liable under the laws of Turkmenistan.
