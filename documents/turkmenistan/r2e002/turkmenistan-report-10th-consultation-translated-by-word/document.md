Nevertheless, the Convention and Recommendation call on States to eliminate and prevent discrimination in education based, in particular, on national origin.14 In this context, a number of Member States that participated in the AdvisoryAnd The Influx  of  Refugees  around  the  world  and  the  emergence  of  serious  educational problems  in this  area,  as  well  as  the  framework  recently  adopted  by  the  international community of 19, demonstrate the ability of States to report on the measures taken to include refugees and migrants in their education systems.

In addition, this aspect is also in line with the 4.5 SDGs, which calls on States to ensure  equal  access  to  all  levels  of  education  and  training  for  vulnerable  groups, including "children in vulnerable situations", and covers most of the objectives of the SDG 4.
