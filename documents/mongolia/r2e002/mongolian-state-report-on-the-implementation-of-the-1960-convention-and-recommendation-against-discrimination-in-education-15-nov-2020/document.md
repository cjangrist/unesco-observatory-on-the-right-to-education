practical obstacles) and on the main issues that need to be addressed for promoting further the right to education in your country (several items could be ticked/mentioned):

☐ Challenges related to equitable access and inclusion in education (such as poverty and socio-economic  factors,  cultural  and  traditional  attitudes,  remoteness,  migration  and displacement, etc.)

One of the urgent problems that need to be solved are educational inequality. That is, the pervasive inequities in access to education. There are several causes to that, including the inequality to wealth and living standards, as well as, the migration of citizens from rural and isolated areas to the capital.

The  aforementioned  challenges  can  be  retaliated  by  developing  an  order  of  priority  in educational sector. Also, by making education an instrument of social policy, by creating an intersectoral collaboration between sectors to facilitate an upgraded educational system, and by increasing parental involvement in the development of a better education for their children.

☐ Challenges related to the quality of education (such as shortages of qualified, trained and motivated teaching staff, non-supportive or inadequate teaching and learning as well as school environment, etc.)

It  is  important  to  raise  and  give  incentives  to  educators and new pedagogical graduates working at schools and kindergartens in remote areas. As well, it is necessary to increase the coverage rate of primary education, to ameliorate basic educational system, to improve the learning environment in schools located in bags (subdistricts), soums and aimags, make e-learning more accessible, to upgrade the educational governance, to reduce the school dropout rates, and to create a lifelong educational system.

These issues are reflected in the main directions of economic and social development of Mongolia and in the strategic plan of "Vision 2050".

☐ Challenges relating to the system and governance (such as budgetary constraints, lack or weakness of monitoring systems - including the lack of reliable data, absence of supportive/ inconsistent/obsolete  legal  and  policy  environment,  lack  of  involvement  of  relevant stakeholders and lack of cooperation/coordination at different levels, etc.)

It is substantial to reduce the political influence on educational system and curricula. That includes, having less political engagements in all decision-making aspects of educational sector. Having less external influence will allow the educational sector to flourish in a non-corrupt and more goal and efficiency-oriented way. Being less dependent from government bodies will create more accountable and responsible staffs. It will also enable to use the budget  efficiently  and  transparently.  Further,  it  will  increase  the  authority  of  educational institutions.

Our challenges also include improving cooperation between educational sector and other sectors. For instance, rapid introduction of electronic learning platforms and the reliability of information and statistics.
