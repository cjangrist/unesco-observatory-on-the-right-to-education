Department of Education

# Niue Education  Strategic Plan   (2005 - 2010)

VISION: Life-Long Learning is the Pathway to Prosperity……Moui Fakaako ko e Halavaka ke Monuina

Preamble:  to provide and sustain a quality relevant and balanced education service for an effective dynamic education service is central to the fulfilment of our unique identity.  It seeks to embrace and nurture the children within a secure learning environment so that as active learners they are healthy, happy and vibrant.  Our education service will enable learners to be responsive to change, to make appropriate moral choices to become life long learners and responsible citizens.  In these ways the achievements of all children will be raised and the aspirations of the nation will be fulfilled.
