# LAW ON CHANGES AND AMENDMENTS OF LAW ON PRIMARY EDUCATION

## Article 1

In  Law  on  Primary  Education  (»Official  Journal  of  the  RM«,  number 64/02), in article 4, paragraph 1 after word »educaion« the words »according

to publicly valid educational curriculum« shall be added.

## Article 2

In article 8 words: »art primary education« shall be replaced by words:

»primary art education«.

## Article 3

In article 11 after the idented line 5 a new idented line 6 shall be added; it reads:

»  -  »official  language«  is  the  language  which  is  in  official  use  in  the Republic;«

In  idented  line  12  afer  word    »school«  words:  »according  to  publicly valid educational curriculum« shall be added.

Former idented lines 6,  7,  8,  9,  10,  11,  12,  13  and  14  shall  become idented lines 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14 and 15.

In article 12 paragraph 1 afer word »basis« words: »publicly valid« shall be added.

After paragraph 2 a new paragraph 3 shall be added; it reads:

»The manner of application and implementation of educational curricula shall be regulated by a regulation of the Ministry«.

## Article 5

In article 12 paragraph 1 words: »educational plan and program« shall

be replaced by words: »educational curriculum«.

In  paragraph  3  words:  »Bureau  for  Educational  Services«  shall  be replaced by words: »Council for General Education«.

In paragraph 4 words: »and the local community« shall be removed.

## Article 6

In  article  23  after  paragraph  2  a  new  paragraph  3  shall  be  added;  it reads:

»Class with the instruction in the language and letter of a minority may have smaller number of pupils which may not be less than 50% of the number of pupils set forth by regulation of  the Ministry«.

In paragraph 3 words: »at the proposal of school« shall be removed.

## Article 7

In  article  25  paragraph  2  after  words  »class«  the  words:  »as  a  rule« shall be added.

In article 4 words: »starting from the first grade or from the sixth grade as a second language« shall be removed.

## Article 8

In  article  26  paragraph  2  words:  »the  Serbian  Language«  shall  be substituted by words: »official language«.

## Article 9

After  article  26  subtitle:  »Schools  for  Teacher  Training  Practice«  and article 27 shall be removed.

## Article 10

After article 27 subtitle:Contract« and article 28 shall be removed.

## Article 11

In  article  33  paragraph  2  shall  change  and  read:  »For  enrollment  of children in school the authority set forth by the statute of school shall appoint the commission«.

## Article 12

In  article  34  after  word  »proposal«  words:  »respectively  with  the consent« shall be added.

## Article 13

In article 48 paragraph 2 after word »subject«  comma shall be inserted instead of fullstop and the following words shall be added: »except of  pupil  of the first grade during the first classification period«.

## Article 14

In article 49 paragraph 1 after word »period« comma shall be inserted instead of fullstop and the following words shall be added: »except for pupils of the first grade at the end of the first classification period«.

## Article 15

In  article  50  after  paragraph  2  a  new  paragraph  3  shall  be  added;  it reads: »The  manner  of  calculating  the    average  of  positive  marks  shall  be

regulated closer by regulation of the Ministry«.

## Article 16

In article 55 paragraph 2 and article 56 paragraph 2 words: »competent Bureau  for  Educational  Services«  shall  be  replaced  by  words:  »Examining Center«.

## Article 17

In  article  72  paragraph  3  words:  »the  competent  Council«  shall  be substituted by word »the Ministry«.

## Article 18

In  article  75  after  paragraph  1  a  new  paragraph  2  shall  be  added;  it reads:

»The manner of implementing the rights and obligations of educator in elementary school shall be regulated by the agreement between the school and the preschool institution«.

Former paragraphs 2 and 3 shall become paragraphs 3 and 4.

## Article 19

In article 76 paragraph 6 shall change and read:

»The teaching process in the fifth grade shall be delivered by grade-teaching instructor, or teacher who has completed twoyears post-secondary school or the Faculty for Teacher Training, except for the instruction of foreign

In article 77 paragraph 3 words: »Serbian Language« shall be replaced by words: »official language«.

## Article 21

In article 79 paragraph 1 idented line 1 words: »Serbian, or« shall be removed. After paragraph 1 a new paragraph shall be added; it reads: »Grade-teaching  instructors  shall,  within    forty-hours  working  week, deliver  20  to  22    lessons  of  immediate  work  with  pupils  in  line  with  general part  of  the  educational  curriculum,  and  the  distribution  of  the  remaining working time, within the forty-hours working week, shall be established by the statute of  school«. In paragraph 2 words: »30 lessons« shall be replaced by words: »not less than 20 lessons«.

Former paragraphs 2 and 3 shall  become paragraphs 3 and 4.

## Article 22

Article 80 shall change and read: »Educators shall, within forty-hours working week, deliver 26  lessons of immediate work with pupils, and the distribution of  the remaining working time shall be established by the statute of school, or preschool institution, in line with the agreement between the school and the preschool institution«.

## Article 23

In  article  84  after  paragraph  2  a  new  paragraph  3  shall  be  added;  it reads: »The teachers who found themselves in school  in a permanent labor relation on the day of coming of this law into force, and who do not have the educational profile set forth in article 76 of this law, shall continue working«.
