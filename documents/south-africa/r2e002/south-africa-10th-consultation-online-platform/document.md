[QG2Q31] Please elaborate further on the measures taken, including those to ensure gender equality and the empowerment of girls and women in and through education:
Through Career Awareness Programmes linked to the Life Orientation learning area, children are exposed to various study funding models and opportunities. All co-curricular and school enrichment activities equitably accommodate girls and boys. The White Paper 6 on Inclusive Education promotes creation of reasonable accommodations for inclusion of learners with diverse identities and abilities in the mainstream schooling system, providing the necessary support through a system-wide approach. Teaching for All, a teacher development programme designed in collaboration with the British Council, has both a pre-service and in-service training component for teachers on inclusivity and diversity management. With TechnoGirl Trust, a TechnoGirl programme is implemented, which seeks to expose girls to STEM careers in the world of work. More and more girls are onboarded and supported in the uptake of STEM gateway subjects at school. A draft policy for the prevention and management of learner pregnancy, dedicated at managing, among other things, the return of girl learners who fell pregnant whilst at school, as soon as it is medically feasible to do so. The same draft policy, which primarily advocate for the delay of sexual debut and prevention of early unintended pregnancies, makes provision for the reasonable support of girls who fall pregnant when they are unable to come to school. In mainstreaming inclusion of children of different abilities, the White Paper 6 advocates for adaptation of education infrastructure, especially in Full Service Schools, so that even learners with physical barriers are able to access all school activities as far as possible. The Flemish Development Agency supports the DBE in the contextualization of a Gender Responsive Pedagogy Toolkit for Early Childhood Education, to support adaptation of teaching methods free from gender binaries and heteronormativity / heterosexism.
