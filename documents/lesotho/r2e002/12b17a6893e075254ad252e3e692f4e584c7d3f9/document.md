# LEROTHOLI POLYTECHNIC ACT, 1997 ACT NO. 1 OF 1998

An act to establish the Lerotholi Polytechnic and to provide for incidental matters. Enacted by the Parliament of Lesotho.

## PART I - PRELIMINARY

### Short title and commencement

- This Act may be cited as the Lerotholi Polytechnic Act 1997 and shall come into operation on the date to be appointed by the Minister by notice in the Gazette.

### Interpretation

- In this Act unless the context otherwise requires-

'Academic Board' means the Academic Board of the Polytechnic established under section 17; 'academic staff' means members of staff of the Polytechnic appointed on academic terms of service  and  includes  the  Director  and  the  Deputy  Directors;  'administrative  staff'  means members of staff of the Polytechnic who have not been appointed on academic terms of service; 'Alumni'  means  former  students  of  Lerotholi  Technical  School,  Lerotholi  Artisan  Training Centre,  Lerotholi  Technical  Institute,  Technician  Training  School  and  Commercial  Training Institute;

'Council' means the Governing Council of the Polytechnic established under section 7;

'Director' means the Director of the Polytechnic;

'Minister' means the Minister responsible for education;

'Ordinances'  means  the  Ordinances  of  the  Polytechnic  made  under  this  Act;  'Polytechnic' means  the  Lerotholi  Polytechnic  established  under  section  3;  ''special  resolution'  means  a resolution passed at the meeting of the Council by a majority of not less than three-fourths of those present and voting;

'Statutes' means the Statutes of the Polytechnic made under the authority of this Act;

'student'  means  a  person  admitted  and  registered  into  an  academic  programme  of  the Polytechnic whether fulltime or part-time;

'University' means any University to which the Polytechnic is affiliated.

## PART II - CONSTITUTION AND GOVERNMENT OF THE POLYTECHNIC

### Establishment of the Polytechnic

(1) There is established the Lerotholi Polytechnic.

- The Polytechnic is a body corporate with perpetual succession and a common seal.

- The Polytechnic is capable of-

(a) suing and being sued;

(b) compounding or proving in any court of competent jurisdiction all debts or sums of

(c) acquiring, holding, dealing with and disposing of movable ,and immovable property;

(d) doing all such other things as are essential, incidental or conducive to the discharge or performance of the functions of the Polytechnic or in order to further the object of the Polytechnic as a place of learning, education and research; and

(e) doing and allowing such other acts and things as bodies corporate may by law do or allow.

### Membership

- The Polytechnic consists of

-

(a) members of the Council;

(b) full-time members of the academic and administrative staff of the Polytechnic;

(c) graduates of the Polytechnic;

(d) students of the Polytechnic; and

(e) such other persons as may, under this Act or the statutes, be admitted to the membership of the Polytechnic.

(1) The functions of the Polytechnic are -

(a) to provide vocational and technical education;

(b) to provide courses of study, instruction or training in fields that the Council may from time to time deem appropriate;

(c) to confer, grant or give or to recommend conferring, granting or giving diplomas, certificates or other educational awards with respect to technical and craft courses;

(d) to provide facilities for, and undertake, research;

(e) to disseminate knowledge and to promote scholarship;

(f) to foster relationships by way of affiliation, association or link with other institutions and individuals;

(g) to enter into arrangements with other relevant institutions for the purposes of offering joint courses and of conducting research and development work; and

(h) to exploit the results of research and development work undertaken by the Polytechnic either separately or jointly, including entering into arrangements with other bodies or persons and participation in limited liability companies for that purpose.

- The Minister may, on the recommendation of the Council, assign to the Polytechnic additional functions.

### Affiliation with other institutions

- The Polytechnic may, with the approval of the Council, affiliate, associate or have academic links with other institutions wherever situated.

### Disqualification from office

(1) The following people are not qualified to be members of the Council:

(a) an unrehabilitated insolvent except with the leave of the High Court;

(b) any person who at any time has been convicted of high treason, sedition, or any crime or offence involving dishonesty or violence to the person of another;

(c) any person suffering from mental incapacity within the meaning of the Mental Health Law, 1964 1

- An appointed member shall vacate his office as an appointed member, if he,

(a) resigns office by writing to the Minister;

(b) becomes an ex-officio member of the Council;

(c) is absent, without leave granted by the Council, from three successive meetings of the Council of which due notice has been given; or

(d) ceases to hold the office or qualification in respect of which the person was appointed a member of the Council.

### Casual appointment

- If  a  vacancy  occurs in the office of an  appointed  member  of  the  Council,  the  Minister  shall appoint a person qualified to fill the vacancy for the remainder of the term of the appointed member and the person appointed to fill the casual vacancy may at the end of the term be reappointed if still eligible.

(1) The Academic Board shall assist the Council and the Director in the planning, coordination and development of the academic activities of the Polytechnic.

(a) to prepare annual, long-term and a five year rolling academic development plan of the Polytechnic;

(b) to design, develop and implement appropriate programmes of study;

(c) to make recommendations to the Council for the establishment of appropriate structures for the implementation of programmes of study;

(d) to direct and regulate the research carried out under the auspices of the Polytechnic;

(e) to make rules for the conduct of examinations and for the evaluation of academic programmes;

(f) to appoint external examiners and academic consultants or advisers;

(g) to make rules for the award of fellowships; scholarships, bursaries, prizes or other awards;

(h) to make general arrangements for tutorial or other academic counselling;

(i) to regulate and control the admission of persons to courses of study in the Polytechnic;

(j) to formulate and promulgate a Code of Conduct to be observed by the students of the Polytechnic and to regulate the discipline of the students;

(k) to recommend to the University the award of certificates; and

- to consider the budgets of departments and schools of the Polytechnic in respect of teaching and research for recommendation to the Council.

- The Academic Board may establish standing or special committees to assist it in the performance of its duties.

- Subject to the provisions of this Act, the Academic Board may regulate its own procedure.

### Employment of staff

(1) The Council may, subject to the approval of the Minister, employ such staff as it thinks appropriate.

- Subject to subsection (3), the terms and conditions of service of the staff of the Polytechnic shall be determined by the Council after consultation with the Academic Board.

### Existing staff

(1) Any person who, immediately before the coming into operation of this Act, was employed full-time  at  the  Lerotholi  Technical  Institute,  Technician  Training  School  or  the Commercial Training Institute, as the case may be, may within the period of six months of  the  coming  into  operation  of  this  Act,  if  he  so  wishes,  elect  to  terminate  his employment with the Public Service and thereafter serve under the Polytechnic.

- A person employed by the Lerotholi Technical Institute, Technician Training School or the  Commercial  Training  Institute  who,  on  or  after  the  commencement  of  this  Act, elects to serve under the Polytechnic shall,

(a) be regarded as an employee of the Polytechnic; and

(b) subject  to  Pensions  Proclamation  1964 2 ,  be  paid  any  pension  and  or  gratuity accruing at the time of his election to serve under the Polytechnic.

### Retirement of Staff

(1) Subject to the provisions of this Act as specified in the Statutes, Academic Staff shall retire from  the  Polytechnic  on  attaining  the  age  of  sixty-five  and  administrative  staff  shall retire from the Polytechnic on attaining the age of sixty.

- An academic staff member may, on or at any time after attaining the age of fifty-five years and subject to three months notice being given, retire or be required to retire.

- An administrative staff member may, on or at any time after attaining the age of fifty years and subject to three months notice being given, retire or be required to retire.

- If,  in  the  opinion  of  the  Council  it  is  in  the  public  interest  to  retain  an  academic  or administrative staff member in his post beyond the age of sixty-five years or sixty years respectively, that member of staff may, if willing, be retained for such a period as the Council may determine.

(a) enabling a member of staff of the Polytechnic to pursue study. or research at any University or elsewhere;

(b) the advancement of learning generally; or

(c) assisting any affiliation, association or connection with another institution,

shall be deemed to be an application of those moneys for the purposes of the Polytechnic.

### Borrowing power

- Subject to the Loans (Statutory Bodies) Act, 1975 3 , the Polytechnic may with the consent of the Minister, raise or borrow money with or without interest and on such terms as may be agreed upon between the lender and the Polytechnic.

### Budget

(1) The Council shall in each financial year adopt for the next financial year, a budget for all funds of the Polytechnic other than those specified in subsection (2) and shall control the  expenditure  of  the  Polytechnic  so  that  it  conforms  as  nearly  as  practicable  to  the approved budget.

- The Council shall review annually funds available to the Polytechnic by way of bequest, donation, or  special  grant,  and  must  subject  to  the  terms  of  any  trust  and  before  any expenditure is made, approve the proposed disposition of these funds.

- The entire  budget  (including  the  subvention,  required  from  the  public  funds)  shall  be submitted to the Minister for the Minister's approval.

- All rights, liabilities and obligations of the Government of Lesotho in respect of the Lerotholi Technical Institute, Technician Training School or the Commercial Training Institute shall be, from the coming into operation of this Act, rights, liabilities and obligations of the Polytechnic and enforceable by or against the Polytechnic accordingly.

### Legal proceedings pending

- Legal  proceedings  pending  immediately  before  the  coming  into  operation  of  this  Act  by  or against  the  Government  in  relation  to  the  Lerotholi  Technical  Institute,  Technician  Training School or  the  Commercial  Training  Institute  may  be  continued  on  or  after  the  coming  into operation of this Act by or against the Polytechnic as the party to the proceedings.

### Regulations

- The  Minister  may  make  Regulations  not  inconsistent  with  the  Act,  prescribing  all  matters which  by  this  Act  are  required  or  permitted  to  be  prescribed  or  which  are  necessary  or convenient to be prescribed for carrying out or giving effect to this Act.
