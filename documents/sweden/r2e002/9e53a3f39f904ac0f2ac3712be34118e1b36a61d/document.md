# The Swedish Higher Education Act (1992:1434)

The Swedish Higher Educaon Act contains provisions about the higher educaon instuons that are accountable to the Government. These provisions are oen supplemented by the regulaons in the Higher Educaon Ordinance.

### Section 1

This act contains provisions relang to higher educaon instuons for which the accountable authority is the Government.

Hereinaer, higher educaon instuon shall refer to universies and university colleges unless otherwise specified. (Ordinance : ).

### Section 4

The operaons of higher educaon instuons shall be arranged to ensure that high standards are aained in courses and study programmes and in research.

The resources available shall be used effecvely to sustain a high standard of operaon.

Quality assurance procedures are the shared concern of staff and students at higher educaon instuons. Ordinance (:).

### Section 4a

Students shall be entled to exert influence over the courses and study programmes at higher educaon instuons.

Higher educaon instuons shall endeavour to enable students to play an acve role in the connued development of courses and study programmes. Ordinance (:).

In the course of their operaons, higher educaon instuons shall promote sustainable development to assure for present and future generaons a sound and healthy environment, economic and social welfare, and jusce.

### Section 8

First-cycle courses and study programmes shall be based fundamentally on the knowledge acquired by pupils in naonal study programmes in the uppersecondary schools or its equivalent. The Government may, however, permit excepons for courses and study programmes in the fine, applied or performing arts.

First-cycle courses and study programmes shall develop:

- the ability of students to make independent and crical assessments,

- the ability of students to idenfy, formulate and solve problems autonomously, and

- the preparedness of students to deal with changes in working life.

In addion to knowledge and skills in their field of study, students shall develop the ability to:

- gather and interpret informaon at a scholarly level,

- stay abreast of the development of knowledge, and

- communicate their knowledge to others, including those who lack specialist knowledge in the field. Ordinance (:).

## Chapter 2 Organisation of public-sector higher education institutions

### Section 1

The Riksdag decides which public-sector higher educaon instuons shall exist.

### Section 2

The board of governors of a higher educaon instuon shall supervise all of its operaons and assume responsibility for the fulfilment of its mandate.

### Section 3

A vice-chancellor who is directly accountable to the board of governors shall be appointed to manage the operaons of each higher educaon instuon.

### Section 4

The Government shall appoint the chair of the board of governors of a higher educaon instuon. The vice-chancellor shall be a member of the board of governors. Teachers and students at the higher educaon instuon shall be entled to representaon on the board of governors. The Government or the authority decided by the Government may, pursuant to Secon  of Chapter of the Instrument of Government, issue regulaons on the number of such members and how they shall be appointed. The Government appoints other members of the board of governors.

## Chapter 4 Students

### Section 1

In so far as this is possible in compliance with the quality requirement laid down in the first paragraph of Secon  of Chapter , higher educaon instuons shall admit students who fulfil the entry requirements for their studies.

### Section 2

Unless otherwise provided by regulaons issued by the Government or the agency nominated by the Government, the higher educaon instuon offering a course or study programme shall decide on its entry requirements. Ordinance (:).

### Section 3

If there are not enough places on a programme for all the qualified applicants, a selecon shall be made. The Government or the agency nominated by the Government may issue regulaons on selecon. Ordinance (:).

### Section 4

Educaon is to be provided without charge to students who are cizens of

. a state that is included in the Agreement on the European Economic Area, or

. Switzerland.

The first paragraph above does not apply to a fee paid in order to enable a student to parcipate in first or second-cycle courses or programmes within the framework of an agreement on educaonal cooperaon between a HEI and HEI outside Sweden if the fee

. is not received by the HEI, and

. is not intended to pay for the secon of a programme arranged by the HEI.

The Government or the agency nominated by the Government issues regulaons on the fees to be charged for students in first and second-cycle courses and programmes who are not cizens of the states laid down in the first paragraph above. Ordinance (:).

A student union shall be democracally organised and able to represent the students within the union's area of acvity. Ordinance (:).

A student union shall submit an annual report on its acvies to the higher educaon instuon and an account of how many students are members.

At the request of the higher educaon instuon, a student union shall provide the instuon with its register of its members. Ordinance (:).
