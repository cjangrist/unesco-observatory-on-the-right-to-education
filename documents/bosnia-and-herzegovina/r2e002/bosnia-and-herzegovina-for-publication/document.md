## VI. Questionnaire

### A. Laws and policies (preamble and paras 4 and 7 of the 1974 Recommendation)

#### A1a. Please describe any specific national or sub-national laws, legislation or legal framework(s) in your country that govern the promotion of global citizenship education (GCED) and/or education for sustainable development (ESD). Explain whether GCED and/or ESD themes 1 are mentioned explicitly or are inferred and what types or levels of education are covered.

Framework laws, adopted at the level of Bosnia and Herzegovina, relating to education address issues  such  as  respect  for  all  human  rights  and  fundamental  freedoms,  including  religious freedoms;  equality  in  the  enjoyment  of  the  right  to  education;  non-discrimination;  respect  for diversity and fostering mutual understanding, tolerance and solidarity among all peoples, nations and communities in Bosnia and Herzegovina and the world.

In  accordance  with  the  legal  mandate,  the  Agency  for  Pre-Primary,  Primary  and  Secondary Education  has  developed  a  Common  Core  Curriculum  defined  on  learning  outcomes,  which contains GCED and ESD topics, and especially within the Common Core Curriculum for Civic Education defined on learning outcomes, as well as  within the field of natural sciences in the Common Core Curriculum for Natural Sciences defined on learning outcomes, Common Core Curriculum for Physics defined on learning outcomes, Common Core Curriculum for Chemistry defined  on  learning  outcomes,  Common  Core  Curriculum  and  programs  for  my  environment, nature and society, nature and biology defined on learning outcomes, Common Core Curricula for my environment, nature and society, society and geography defined on learning outcomes, as well as  within  pre-school  upbringing  and  education  in  the  Common  Core  complete  development program for preschool education, defined on learning outcomes.

The  Agency  also  has  a  representative  for  the  UNECE  Strategy  for  Education  for  Sustainable Development who regularly participates in the process of preparing reports and meetings. (United Nations  Economic  Commission  for  Europe  Steering  Committee  on  Education  for  Sustainable Development).

### B. Curriculum (paras 4 and 10-19 of the 1974 Recommendation )

#### B1a. Please describe in which ways GCED and ESD are mainstreamed in school curricula. Indicate  whether  topics  are  taught  as  standalone  subjects  or  are  integrated  into  the curricula of other subjects. Explain whether GCED and/or ESD themes 3 are mentioned explicitly or are inferred and what types or levels of education are covered.

The subject of civic education has been introduced as a compulsory subject in all primary and secondary schools under the name "Democracy and Human Rights" or "Civic Education", and through adapted activities it is also implemented in preschool institutions.  The topics of global citizenship  are  integrated  in  the  curricula  in  several  subjects  from  the  social  field  in  primary schools. In secondary schools, the topics of global citizenship are integrated into a subject called "Democracy  and  Human  Rights"  or  "Politics  and  Civil  Society,  Economy"  or  "Culture  of Religions",  and  in  certain  administrative  units,  contents  on  the  topic  of  global  citizenship  are integrated into the elective subject History of religions".

Contents on the topic of education for sustainable development are integrated into the following subjects in the curricula for primary education: grades 1-4 within the subject "My environment", in grade 5 within the subject "Nature", and from 6 to 9 classes within the subject "Biology". In secondary school curricula, the topics of education for sustainable development are integrated into the subject "Biology", but certain topics, such as sustainable consumption and production, can be integrated into other subjects. Topics of global citizenship and sustainable development can also be discussed in head teacher classes, and are an integral part of various extracurricular activities, including debate sections, environmental sections, and various actions (eg cleaning actions, paper collection for recycling, etc.).

When  it  comes  to  literature,  there  are  ten  titles,  approved  workbooks,  textbooks,  manuals, worksheets and exercise books for primary schools, gymnasiums and secondary technical and vocational schools in the 2020/2021 school year relating to democracy and human rights:

It includes equality and non-discrimination, the value of human rights, dignity, justice, inclusion and participation, and respect for human rights and freedoms.

It is important to emphasize that all subjects unite the educational dimension of student personality development so that within all subjects, more or less, advocate and address topics related to GCED and ESD.

### C. Teacher education (including the training of trainers and educators) (paras 4 and 33-37 of the 1974 Recommendation )

#### C1a. Please describe in which ways GCED and ESD are mainstreamed in initial training and in  continuing  professional  development  of  teachers,  trainers  and  educators.  Indicate whether all teachers, trainers and educators or only some are required to receive training to teach GCED and/or ESD.

Contents related to global citizenship are integrated into initial teacher education programs for social sciences (see answer to question B1a), while contents on sustainable development education are integrated into initial teacher education for science subjects (see answer to question B1a). When it comes to continuous professional development of teachers, many professional trainings are organized on the topics of global citizenship for teachers of all profiles, while the topic of education for sustainable development is more represented in professional training for teachers of

certain profiles (eg biology teachers).

Pedagogical institutes in Bosnia and Herzegovina continuously organize and implement various types of professional development of teachers, professional associates and school management (seminars  /  webinars,  round  tables,  advisory  work,  etc.).  GCED  and  ESD  topics  are  covered through various seminars attended by primary and secondary school teachers. Also, teachers are continuously individually and professionally improved within the direct work with students from GCED and ESD who are integrated in the current curricula.

Also, trainings from GCED or ESD are mandatory for those teachers who teach specific subjects in this field, and they did not have them at the universities. For example, teachers who want to teach Democracy and Human Rights in secondary schools, or Civic Education in primary schools, if they have not taken the subject in college must be trained to be able to teach the subject.

The Agency for Preschool, Primary and Secondary Education has prepared a document "Model for  improving  the  system  of  continuous  professional  development  of  educators,  teachers  and professional associates in Bosnia and Herzegovina" which is intended for ministries of education and pedagogical institutes as a basis for drafting regulations in accordance  with legal competencies.

#### C1b. Please provide examples of teacher training policies or curricula in which GCED and/or ESD are  mainstreamed  and,  where  possible,  web  links  to  relevant  documents. If  possible, include examples related to initial training and to continuing professional development.

In Bosnia and Herzegovina, there is a continuous professional development of teachers, both by pedagogical institutes and by various international governmental and non-governmental organizations.

In the past period, several projects were implemented that included professional development of a large number of primary and secondary school teachers in the following topics: Education for Peace, Inclusive Education, Violence Prevention, Education for Social Justice, Inclusion Index, Life Skills Development, etc.

Some of the above topics are planned and implemented within the Annual Work Program of the school (peace week, violence revention, protection of children's rights…)

### F. Technical and vocational education and training (paras 25-29 of the 1974 Recommendation )

#### F1 Please describe any major initiatives that have been undertaken to promote GCED and/or ESD in technical and vocational education and training. Describe briefly the outcomes and impact of such initiatives and provide examples of good practices.

The promotion of GCED and ESD is carried out within the valid curricula for gimnasiums, secondary vocational and technical schools, the contents of GCED and ESD are realized.

Certain directions of secondary vocational education lasting four years (eg secondary schools of economics) have general education subjects that integrate certain contents of GCED and / or ESD.

In secondary vocational education, as well as in general education, students attend Democracy and Human Rights and Entrepreneurship classes. Outcomes are the creation of civic awareness, respect for human rights, starting an entrepreneurship, etc. Examples of good practice are examining social problems and offering solutions through projects, as well as participation in various events such as fairs and the like.

Insight into the Annual Work Programs of Secondary Schools shows that at a large number of schools activities are implemented that advocate topics from GCED and ESD through the work of the Student Council, the work of the Parents' Council, the implementation of projects with the NGO sector, etc.
